Time-frequency maps

Both maps show the phase relative to the q/T carrier at each pixel's time, blank where the modulus is below the chosen fraction of the map's peak. The STFT uses a Hann window of L samples; the WDM (a = 1/3) has channel spacing n/L bins, the STFT frequency step, so its time pixels are L/2 samples. The WDM coefficients are real, one quadrature of the complex z whose phase is shown. Its first and last pixels light up across the band: the transform is periodic in time and the 2-yr boxcar is a jump there.
