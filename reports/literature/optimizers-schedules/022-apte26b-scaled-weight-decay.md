# Scale Weight Decay and Train Better (Apte et al., 2026)

[arXiv:2607.23777](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23777), preprint.

**Evidence: C.** Not peer-reviewed; 2 citations in 0.2 yr (Semantic Scholar, 2026-10-19). Its
claims are the authors' alone: a hypothesis, not a result.

## What they do

- Muon-SW: scale the weight decay so that the weight RMS stays constant through training,
  instead of drifting down.

## What they find

- About 30% fewer steps to a given loss.

## Limits

- LLMs only.
