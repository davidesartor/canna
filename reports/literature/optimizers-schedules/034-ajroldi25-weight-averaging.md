# When, Where and Why to Average Weights? (Ajroldi et al., 2025)

[arXiv:2502.06761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06761), preprint.

**Evidence: B.** Peer-reviewed (International Conference on Machine Learning); 7 citations in
1.7 yr (Semantic Scholar, 2026-10-19).

## What they do

- Test weight averaging across the AlgoPerf workloads.

## What they find

- Averaging speeds up training on every workload and slightly improves generalisation.
- It complements lr decay rather than replacing it; the best results combine both.
