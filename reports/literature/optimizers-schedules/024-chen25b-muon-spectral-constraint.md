# Muon Optimizes Under Spectral Norm Constraints (Chen et al., 2025)

[arXiv:2506.15054](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15054), TMLR 2026.

**Evidence: A.** Peer-reviewed (Trans. Mach. Learn. Res.); 92 citations in 1.3 yr (Semantic
Scholar, 2026-10-19).

## What they do

- Analyse Muon with decoupled weight decay as an implicit constrained problem.

## What they find

- Muon + decay solves a spectral-norm-constrained problem with radius ∝ 1/λ.
- So λ sets a ceiling on every Muon matrix's spectral norm.
