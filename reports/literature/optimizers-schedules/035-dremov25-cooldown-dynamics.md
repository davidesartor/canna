# Training Dynamics of the Cooldown Stage in Warmup-Stable-Decay Learning Rate Scheduler (Dremov et al., 2025)

[arXiv:2508.01483](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01483), TMLR.

**Evidence: B.** Peer-reviewed (Trans. Mach. Learn. Res.); 12 citations in 1.2 yr (Semantic
Scholar, 2026-10-19).

## What they do

- Study the cooldown of WSD schedules: its shape and the optimizer settings during it.

## What they find

- The cooldown shape trades bias against variance.
- A higher β₂ during the cooldown consistently helps.

## Limits

- LLMs only.
