# Understanding and Improving Shampoo and SOAP via Kullback-Leibler Minimization (Lin et al., 2025)

[arXiv:2509.03378](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.03378), preprint.

**Evidence: C.** Not peer-reviewed; 17 citations in 1.1 yr (Semantic Scholar, 2026-10-19). Its
claims are the authors' alone: a hypothesis, not a result.

## What they do

- Recast the second-moment estimation of Shampoo and SOAP as KL covariance estimation.

## What they find

- KL-Shampoo needs no Adam grafting, costs about as much per step as SOAP, and beats it.

## Limits

- No JAX implementation known.
