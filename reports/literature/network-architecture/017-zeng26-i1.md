# i1: A Simple and Fully Open Recipe for Strong Text-to-Image Models

Zeng et al., 2026 · [arXiv:2606.11289](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11289)

**Evidence: C.** Not peer-reviewed; 3 citations in 0.4 yr (Semantic Scholar, 2026-10-19). Its
claims are the authors' alone: a hypothesis, not a result.

## What they do

More than 300 controlled T2I ablations at XL/2 scale.

## What they find

- Backbones, DPG / PRISM: cross-attention (0.89B) 84.66 / 56.4; single-stream (0.82B) 85.89 / 55.6; dual-stream (1.24B) 86.82 / 58.3.
- A one-block, 17M-parameter text adapter adds about 1–1.7 DPG to every backbone.
- Removing adaLN: dual-stream 86.82 → 87.82.
- Removing t is neutral once an adapter is present.
- Long skips help dual-stream (figures only).

## Implementation details

A dedicated condition-only pre-block (adapter). Long skips.

## Caveats

T2I metrics. Many results are figure-only.
