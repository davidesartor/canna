# Rotary Position Embedding for Vision Transformer

Heo et al., 2024, ECCV 2024 · [arXiv:2403.13298](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13298)

**Evidence: A.** Peer-reviewed (European Conference on Computer Vision); 325 citations in 2.6
yr (Semantic Scholar, 2026-10-19).

## What they do

RoPE-Mixed, with learned cross-axis frequencies.

## What they find

Learned frequencies barely change in-distribution accuracy (ViT-S 80.9 → 80.7). They help extrapolation by +1.7; Mixed by +3.0.
