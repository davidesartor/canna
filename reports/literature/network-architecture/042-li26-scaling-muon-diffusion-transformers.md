# Scaling Muon for Diffusion Transformers

Li et al., 2026 · [arXiv:2608.20818](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20818)

**Evidence: C.** Not peer-reviewed; 0 citations in 0.2 yr (Semantic Scholar, 2026-10-19). Its
claims are the authors' alone: a hypothesis, not a result.

## What they do

Muon at 1.3–15B DiT scale.

## What they find

FD-DINO from AdamW to Muon: 53.93 → 46.08 … 40.23 → 33.51 across sizes. Row-normalization alone, without Newton–Schulz, is much worse: 51.32 vs 41.73.
