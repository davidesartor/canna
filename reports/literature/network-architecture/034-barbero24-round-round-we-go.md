# Round and Round We Go! What makes Rotary Positional Encodings useful?

Barbero et al., 2024, ICLR 2025 · [arXiv:2410.06205](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06205)

**Evidence: A.** Peer-reviewed (International Conference on Learning Representations); 133
citations in 2.0 yr (Semantic Scholar, 2026-10-19).

## What they do

The highest RoPE frequencies build positional heads; the lowest carry semantics (p-RoPE).

## What they find

Removing the lowest 25% of frequencies changes perplexity only in the third decimal.
