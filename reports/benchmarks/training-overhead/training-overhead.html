What do restarts and checkpoints cost a training run?

Timed on the 10-22 network (2026-10-24, src at 54f11d7 or earlier, before the catalogue prior, unordered sources, the x1 head and the per-source frame). The scripts follow HEAD (its history keys, diagnostics(problem, state, ...)) and were not rerun. The batch was the old default of 256 observations (4096 samples); HEAD's is 1024 samples.

Training runs as a chain of 24 h jobs. Each job pays a restart (uv, imports, a cold restore, compiles), a per-epoch cost every log interval (diagnostics, history.npz, the checkpoint) and the half epoch the time limit kills. Measured in the trainer's own loop on its 2 CPUs at batch 256; the chunk model adds up the parts.

Every trainer-loop series of this config; the selected one is bold. Dots mark each series' best interval; click anywhere on a curve to set the interval.

Where does a 24 h job go besides steps?

The lost share of one chunk at the chosen interval: the 300 s requeue margin, the restart, the per-epoch costs and the killed epoch (the whole epoch when the kill lands inside its background write).

What does a restart cost?

Before the first step: the uv run start, the imports with the git check and setup, a cold restore of the checkpoint, and the diagnostics and epoch compiles. ≥ marks lower bounds: the record ran after other configs in one process and reused their compiles.

What has been measured?

Share of a 24 h job lost at the chosen interval, with the restart below it, for every series and config. OOM: the trainer loop ran out of memory (in the epoch, the setup or the diagnostics); —: not run.

What this means

  • Restarts and checkpoints cost 1.2–2.3% of each 24 h job at the default interval of 1000, with the bf16 backbone: a B run takes 1.03–1.04 d on the A100 80 GB and 1.2–1.6 d on the L40S.
  • The restart is mostly compiles on the job's 2 CPUs (73–96% of 400–705 s). The async save blocks for under a second; its write overlaps the next epoch.
  • The log interval is not worth tuning with a fast step: the best interval saves at most 0.6 points over 1000. With bf16 attention a 1000-step epoch takes hours, and 50–200 is the right interval.
  • XL is borderline in the trainer's own process: full-XL on the A100 80 GB and narrow-XL on the L40S ran out of memory in the loop although they fit alone in the step check.
