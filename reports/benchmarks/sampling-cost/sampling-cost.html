What does one posterior cost, flow against MCMC?

Timed on the 10-22 network. Measured 2026-10-24, before the catalogue prior, unordered sources, the x1 head and the per-source frame. The scripts follow HEAD (sample_posterior(problem, flow, z, y, f), 64 calls per draw, no self-conditioning) and were not rerun, so HEAD may be slightly cheaper per draw.

The flow samples a window's posterior on an A100 80 GB with the bf16 backbone: 32 midpoint ODE steps, so 64 network calls per draw (the timings were taken on the 10-22 network, which also ran self-conditioning: 65 calls per draw). The MCMC reference runs on 2 CPU cores per chain. The hardware differs: read the gap as the cost of a result, not as a like-for-like speedup.

What this means

  • A posterior from the flow costs a fraction of a second. 256 draws of one window take 0.22 s (narrow-B) and 0.25 s (full-B), warm on an A100 80 GB, linear in draws, and 9–10× the time from XS to XL for 51× the parameters.
  • Compiling dominates a handful of windows. The first call takes 27–37 s; after that the flow does a window in under a second at every size.
  • MCMC costs hours per window. 3.4–5.7 h per window joint, on 2 CPU cores: the median is 59,000–66,000× the flow at size B, as the cost of a result rather than a like-for-like speedup.
