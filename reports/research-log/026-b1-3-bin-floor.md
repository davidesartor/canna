# F26 (2026-10-10): B1 — B trains cleanly, but f₀ stops at ~3 bins: XS's floor, in coordinate units

- B trains without trouble, but only 1 of 240 sources is under a bin: widths 2.8–4.5 bins at
  every SNR.
- That is XS's floor expressed in the flow's f₀ coordinate (~2×10⁻³ units of x_f), not bf16
  input cells, token positions or eval rounding.
- The fix is architectural (representation of position, or bf16 in training), testable on XS.

**Training** (jobs 13882121 → 13882126). `B-late`, 512 × 8, 200k steps, last 40k cooling.
- Four 24 h links; each resumed within seconds after the limit at epochs 56, 112, 167. 1.54 s
  per step, 18.6 GiB peak, no NaN.
- Restarting the optimizer per link cost nothing visible (0.5370 → 0.5374 at epoch 57, 0.5154 →
  0.5098 at 113).
- 10-epoch flow loss: 0.668 (1–10), 0.538 (51–60), 0.525 (91–100), **0.498** (151–160, last
  constant lr), **0.473** (191–200). Cooldown worth −0.025 vs ~−0.012 from the trend. XS ended
  at 0.435 (512) and 0.428 (768).
- Aux losses drift once their weight hits 0 (y 1508, x 0.25), as on XS; sampling uses only the
  velocity.

- **Broad but honest:** in68 0.84–0.90, in95 0.97–1.00; offsets ~0.4 widths.
- **Offsets are shared within an injection:** per-injection-mean std 3.2 bins vs 1.6 within.
  The loudest eval injection (q1.00) is shifted +12 to +19 bins on all four sources. Below 1
  mHz widths and offsets are ~20% larger.
- **Loudest source (SNR 1668, `corner/gb-loudest.pdf`):** sky resolved (λ ±0.009 rad), M_c
  broad (±0.11), ψ and φ₀ flat while Fisher has them tight — expected with f₀ off by 3 bins.
  Other pages: median (SNR 60.8, q0.10 s3) and faintest (SNR 7.3, q0.20 s1). The "Too few
  points to create valid contours" warnings are cosmetic.

**Ruled out** (positions regenerated by `outputs/scorecards/positions_B.py`):
- **bf16 cell of the input coordinate:** 6 bins for |x_f| > 0.5, smaller toward the centre, but
  sources in 0.09–0.4-bin cells are as wide (2.5–3.0) as in 6-bin cells (≈ 3.2); Spearman ρ
  +0.21.
- **bf16 y-token positions:** `PositionalEmbed`'s bf16 `arange(2048)` gives only **641 distinct
  positions** (steps of 2/4/8 tokens above 256/512/1024; XS and S are exact), and its highest
  frequency is one period per window (0.003 rad between neighbours on B vs 0.2 on XS). Yet
  widths are 3.1, 3.1, 3.5 bins where the step is 4, 8, 16 bins. Still a defect the next B run
  should not keep.
- **Eval-time rounding (bound from F21):** fp32 at eval left XS-late unchanged (0.991, 95% ≥
  0.978), capping it at 7.5×10⁻⁴ units, i.e. ≤ ~7% on B. Not worth running (it would also need
  smaller chunks).

**What fits: a fixed precision in x_f** (window interior → [−1, 1]; one unit = **24 bins on XS,
~1536 on B**):

| run | loud f₀ width (bins) | in units of x_f |
|---|---|---|
| XS-late-768-cool (F23) | 0.037 | **1.5×10⁻³** |
| XS-late-cool (F24, 512) | 0.039 | **1.6×10⁻³** |
| B-late (512, SNR ≥ 40) | 3.0 | **2.0×10⁻³** (IQR 1.7–2.4) |

- With a 64× wider window and a sixth of the steps, B is within 30% of XS's best — about half a
  bf16 ulp of an O(1) number (2⁻⁸ = 3.9×10⁻³). F6/F23's 25× Fisher floor is the same limit; B's
  window makes it 3 bins.
- Finding sources on B (< 1 bin) needs 6.5×10⁻⁴ units, 2.5× finer than XS's best; Fisher for
  loud sources needs ~10⁻⁶.
- On XS only the cooldown has moved it (3.6 → 1.5×10⁻³); width (F19, F24), integrator steps
  (F16), fp32 eval (F21) did not.

**Candidate causes, both structural:**
1. **Representation:** x_f enters `x_embed` as a raw scalar through an MLP, no Fourier
   features; y positions come from sinusoids no finer than the window. Both resolve a fixed
   fraction of the window.
2. **bf16 in training** (F5, F21, open): after the first layer the coordinate lives in O(1)
   bf16 activations, and the gradient can't shape structure below their rounding.

Fourier features of x_f in fp32, periods down to ~1 bin, address both.

**Consequences.** More steps or the 768 width cannot close a 1000× gap. The paper's claim of
one network for the whole band is not supported yet. The fix is architectural and XS can test
it cheaply.
