How does the flow behave on windows it was not trained on?

Each case takes the in-distribution injections and changes one thing: the noise level, one source just outside the prior, a source fewer or more, or no source at all. The window, truth and noise realisation stay the same, so every source pairs with its own in-distribution posterior. Only the flow is used: the data come from the repo's simulator with modified parameters, the posterior from the eval's sampler.

What does the flow claim where there is nothing to find?

Each dot is one of the flow's slots in one window: the median SNR its draws would have (against the trained PSD) and the spread of its f0 draws as a fraction of the window's drawable band. A slot that found a source sits low and to the right; a slot spread uniformly over the window sits near 0.37 (1.4826 × MAD of a uniform). With no source or one missing, a slot has nothing to find: does it still claim a confident source?
