What this means

  • No run yet. The page holds the mock ladder only (src at 8310cb3, 2026-10-30). Run rows drop in once a run has an eval of the current layout (crps_fdot in place of crps_mc); the 2026-10-24 dusty-otter-narrow-XS files stay in data/ but are not shown.
  • Every eval source is faint (the mocks ran with visible_snr = 0, catalogue windows; ŝ at most 2.6), so the prior mock (the skill floor) scores about as well as fisher, and skill is undefined (floor and fisher differ by under 10%). The ladder is read as ratios to fisher; rerun with visible_snr on.
  • The energy score ranks the ladder by magnitude. Narrow band, ratio to fisher: wide 1.53, narrow 1.03, biased 1.04, phases-prior 1.00, loud-floor 1.00 (equal to fisher: no source reaches its cap). The prior mock scores below fisher (0.96; z± CRPS 0.43–0.47, fdot 0.89), because at faint SNR the Fisher mock is a worse posterior than the prior; only its f0 CRPS (1.11) is higher.
  • biased is seen by the f0 CRPS only (1.32 narrow, 1.20 full); the energy score barely moves (1.04, 1.02). phases-prior is invisible to every score on the narrow band (z± CRPS 0.97–1.01).
  • The width ratio separates over-wide from over-narrow (narrow-mock median 5.3 against fisher 15.0; wide 79) but its geometric mean collapses to 0 for wide, since 57 of 800 slots have a zero f0 width; read its median.
  • Caveat. The scores are in unweighted flow coordinates, so the energy score is dominated by the large-scale coordinates (sky, amplitudes); read it with the per-coordinate CRPS.
