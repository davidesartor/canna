      Against the MCMC

      What the checks are
      • Rank calibration (SBC): for a calibrated posterior the truth's rank among the draws is uniform. Per quantity and column, the p of Säilynoja+22's γ test of that uniformity; ✕ marks p below the level. Columns are the slots (sources loudest first), each source's quantity on its own, and the joint draw, a statistic of all sources at once. TARP is the rank in distance to a random reference point; band depth and average rank rank how central the truth is among the draws, so there a high rank is a central truth, a too-wide posterior.
      • Mean rank − ½: > 0 when the truth sits above the draws. Share in the middle half: > ½ when the posterior is too wide, < ½ when too narrow.
      • ECDF − z: the ranks' empirical CDF minus the uniform's at fractional rank z, with the simultaneous band the γ test accepts; a curve above zero at small z has too many low ranks (the truth below the draws).
      • SNR bands are of the draws' median SNR, a function of the data alone, so stratifying by them keeps each stratum's ranks uniform under calibration.
      • Proper scores: the energy score of the draws against the truth, per source and for the joint draw, and the CRPS per coordinate; lower is better, and only comparable between evals of the same injections.
      • Scorecard: random and eval injections scored on f₀ alone by true SNR, the eval job's quick look; against the MCMC: the eval injections' joint MCMC, all sources free.
