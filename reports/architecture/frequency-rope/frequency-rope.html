What does the position kernel resolve, and where does it alias?

With q = k (all ones, every pair aligned) the logit is the mean over pairs of cos(2π Δ/P). This is the part of a score that depends on position only; learned q, k reweight the pairs. Dotted lines are multiples of the token spacing.

Which pairs can tell data tokens apart?

Data tokens sit on a lattice spaced by the patch. A pair whose period divides the patch (patch/P an integer) gives every data token the same angle, so it adds one constant to all of a source's data logits and softmax ignores it. Bar height is the distance of patch/P from the nearest integer; 0 at a period of at most two tokens means blind. For longer periods the bar is just the phase advance per token (patch/P turns): slow, not blind.

How far is the source position from the target along the path?

The source token's RoPE position comes from x_t, not the prediction. Along x_t = x1 + (1 − s)(x0 − x1), x0 and x1 two prior draws, this is |bins(x_t) − bins(x1)|.

What this means

  • Attention places a source to about one token. The aligned kernel has a half width of 2.4 bins on narrow and 4.4 on full, and its first zero is at 17 and 43 bins. Sources in visible windows are loud (median SNR 1.6 narrow, 7.2 full), so their Fisher widths in f0 are narrow: a median 0.36 bins (narrow) and 0.090 (full), 5–95% 0.11–0.82 and 0.036–0.40. All 256 sources per band are narrower than the kernel's half width, and 250 narrow and 256 full are under one bin.
  • Fine f0 is needed by most sources. The finest f0 period is 1/64 bin and no Fisher width is below it (minimum 0.056 narrow, 0.020 full). bf16 position noise is 2.2e-3 (narrow) and 2.4e-3 (full) bins, also under every width.
  • A source's own token wins. Its margin over the best other token is 0.82 on narrow and 0.70 on full at the token centre, and it falls to a tie only at the token edge. The kernel goes negative past its first zero (down to -0.30 and -0.38).
  • Many rope pairs are shorter than a token (14 of 32 narrow, 11 full), and 18 and 14 are under two tokens, so they alias across data tokens; 4 narrow and 2 full pairs have a period that divides the patch.
  • Source positions come from x_t; on full the path error exceeds the median Fisher width. At 1-s = 1e-3 the median position error is 0.11 bins (99%: 0.35) on full and 0.028 (0.086) on narrow; the median Fisher width is 0.36 (narrow) and 0.090 (full) bins.
