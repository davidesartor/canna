Where do the source slots attend?

Each block's softmax for the source-slot queries of a trained LisaFlow (the sampler's self-conditioned pass), recomputed from the block's own q and k on fresh training observations at fixed path times t. Keys are the source slots, the sink tokens and the data tokens; what the data tokens are worth to the output is in data dependence.

Where does a slot's attention go, block by block?

At the selected t: each block's attention mass from a slot to itself, to the other slots, to the sinks and to the data tokens. Mean over observations, slots and the selected heads.

Does each slot attend to its own source's tokens?

Of a slot's attention to the data, the share on the tokens that hold of its own source's power, against t, one line per block (darker is deeper). Dashed: the share uniform attention would give (those tokens' fraction of the window).

How much attention goes to the sinks, and when?

The slots' attention mass on the sink tokens against t, one line per block.

How focused is each head?

Entropy of a slot's attention row over its keys, divided by the log of their number (1: uniform; 0: one key), at the selected t; mean over observations and slots. Darker is more focused.

Does a louder source get more of its slot's attention?

Own-band share of the data attention at the selected t by the source's SNR (all slots pooled), one line per block; selected heads.

What does one observation's attention look like?

One observation at the selected t and block: each slot's attention per data token, mean over heads (darker is more, scaled to the block's largest weight). Ticks above a row mark its own source's tokens, the triangle its f0 token.

What this means

  • No trained run has been measured yet. dusty-otter-narrow-XS (2,000 steps) only checks the pipeline end to end; its maps belong to a network that has barely trained and say nothing about the architecture.
  • Own-band share well above its uniform baseline, rising with t and depth, means slots find their own sources through the f0 RoPE: the attention does the source separation. Near-baseline share in every block means each slot reads the window as a whole, and separation must happen in the MLPs or not at all.
  • Most of the mass on the sinks in a block means that block's attention is mostly switched off for the sources (the sinks are where a head parks unused mass). A block that is all sink at every t is a candidate to drop; one that turns on only late is resolving the posterior's last decades.
  • Low data entropy (well under 1) is a head that reads a few tokens; entropy near 1 reads all of them evenly. Heads that stay at 1 at every t carry no data selection.
  • Whether that attention matters for the output is the data-dependence report's question (the velocity's sensitivity to the data), and whether the output is right is reference comparison's.
