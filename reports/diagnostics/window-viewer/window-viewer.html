How loud is each source, alone and in the data?

Bars: each source's own SNR ρ = ‖ỹ‖ (source_snrs), and the window's, ‖Σỹ‖ (snr), whose tick is √Σρ², what it would be if the sources shared no bins. Diamonds: the matched-filter SNR of the source's own template on the data, ⟨d, ỹ⟩/‖ỹ‖. With the noise off it moves off ρ only through the other sources' overlap; with the noise on it also scatters by about ±1.

What do the tokens of the whole window hold?

The data tokens (stft_tokens) of the data. Each column is one token: its patch bins taken back to the time domain by an orthonormal inverse DFT, so the rows run through the observation, from 0 at the bottom to T at the top. The token holds asinh|z| and the gated phase z/√(1+|z|²) of all three channels; this view shows the selected channel. Noise has E|z|² = 2. The ticks are the sources' f₀, in their colours.
