Posterior validation tests and their power

Every test of canna.lisa.validate, first on the real injected truth, then on an exact null and known errors of the draws, to show which errors each test can see.

Tests on the injected truth

p-value of the ECDF γ test per slot (sources sorted loudest first) and on the whole window (joint), within a stratum of the draws' median SNR. Fill: −log₁₀ p (dark = strong evidence against calibration); outlined: rejected at α. Hover for the mean rank offset (−: truth below the draws) and the share of ranks in the middle half (0.5 when calibrated; above: draws too wide, below: too narrow).

Size and power of each test

Share of 100-injection chunks (and slots, for per-slot tests) in which a test rejects at α = 0.05, with the draws perturbed as each column says. The first column is the exact null: it should read α for the rank tests. Score tests compare each perturbed set's score to the unperturbed draws' (one-sided paired Wilcoxon), so their null column is 0 by construction. toy: linear-Gaussian slots with exact posterior draws; run: one flow draw stands in for the truth, so the null holds whatever the flow's error.
