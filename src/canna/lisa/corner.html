The two coordinate systems
  • Physical: f₀ in frequency bins from the window's first bin (per source: offset from the truth), the chirp mass Mc in M☉, log₁₀ of the strain amplitude A, the ecliptic longitude λ and latitude β (rotated from the simulator's equatorial RA and Dec by the J2000 obliquity, 23.4393°; λ unwrapped around the truth), the polarisation ψ, the inclination ι (sin ι uniform in [−1, 1]) and the initial phase φ₀. ψ and φ₀ are folded onto the copy nearest the truth by the likelihood's exact symmetries ψ → ψ + π and (ψ, φ₀) → (ψ + π/2, φ₀ + π).
  • Flow: the 8 coordinates the network learns (chart.physical_to_flow): xf = (2 ln f₀ − ln flo − ln fhi) / (ln fhi − ln flo) over the window's drawable f₀ range (one unit is half of it); m = (ln Mc − μ) / σ under the chirp-mass prior; galactic l and b over the galactic sky prior's stds; and the helicity amplitudes z± = asinh(A / A₁ · (1 ∓ sin ι)² / 4) · ei(φ₀′ ± 2ψ), A₁ the amplitude of SNR ≈ 1 and φ₀′ the phase at mid-observation. They need no folding: every (ψ, φ₀) copy has the same z±.
  • Sets: the flow's draws matched onto the injected sources, the Fisher forecast (Hessian averaged over noise, plus the prior) and the narrowband MCMC (one source free, the others at their truth), each thinned as the header says. Contours hold 39% and 86% of each set (1σ and 2σ of a 2-D Gaussian); widths are 1.4826 × MAD.
