\section{Conclusions and Discussion}
\label{sec:conclusions}
% =======================================================================================

Conditional flow matching returns joint posteriors of overlapping \acp{gb} under the full
instrument response, with no likelihood evaluation and in a fraction of a second per window. The
chart, the frequency order and the frame turn a posterior that is multimodal by symmetry and
decades narrower than its prior into one that a flow on straight paths can learn, and the one-way
network makes each draw cost only the source side. On the core subproblem with two sources per
window the posteriors have the widths of a joint \ac{mcmc}; they widen as sources are added, and
over the whole band the loudest sources are not yet resolved.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
\label{sec:limitations}
The flow falls short of the chains where the posterior is narrowest. In the narrow band its $f_0$
widths reach \ResultsNarrowEightFzeroLow{} times the chains' with eight sources per window, and the
sky two to three times above $\snr=40$. In the full band the $f_0$ of sources above $\snr=40$ is
\ResultsFullFourFzeroLoud{} to \ResultsFullSixteenFzeroLoud{} times too wide, and with $S=16$ only
\qty{\ResultsFullSixteenFoundLoud}{\percent} of them are found. The misses lie above
\qty{\ResultsFullSplitMhz}{\milli\hertz}, where training sees few loud sources and the flow refines
its frame least (\cref{sec:results-full}). In every model the drawn signals fit the data slightly
worse than the true one, which the marginal widths do not show (\cref{sec:results-calibration}).

The evidence has limits of its own. Our tests are necessary conditions; only the reference chains
give widths, they exist for \EvaluationInjections{} windows per model, and for the faint sources of
the largest problems they are themselves unreliable (\cref{sec:validation}). Every result comes from
one training run per configuration, so the run-to-run spread is not measured; a second seed, the
narrow band with $S=2$ first, would show which differences are real (\cref{app:results-forks}).
Out of distribution, the flow tolerates sources past its prior but turns confident and wrong when
the noise is louder than its assumed \ac{psd} (\cref{app:evaluation-sampler}). Finally, the
simulator is simpler than real \ac{lisa} data, and it generates the training data, the test windows
and the reference chains' likelihood alike. Its simplifications therefore limit realism, not the
consistency of the comparison.

\paragraph{Proposed follow-ups.}
\label{sec:followups}
The first step is realistic orbits, with unequal and time-varying arms and \ac{tdi} generation 2
(\cref{app:forward-model-tdi}), and data gaps. We expect both to enter through the training
simulations, with no change to the cost of a draw or to how its posteriors are checked. A sampler
needs each effect written into its likelihood: gaps break the stationarity that makes
\cref{eq:loglik} diagonal, and whatever the likelihood leaves out biases the chains
\citep{baghi2019gravitational,dey2021effect}.

The second step is the window, which now has a fixed length and exactly $S$ binaries. A global fit
needs a number of sources that follows the density of the Galaxy, a length that follows the width
of the responses, and sources that straddle an edge or leak in from a neighbour; windows also
overlap, a stride of \ForwardModelWindowStride{} responses apart, so each source is inferred in
several and their posteriors must be combined \citep{littenberg2020global}. Beyond these come the
confusion foreground of the unresolved binaries
\citep{timpano2006characterizing,karnesis2021characterization} and the other source classes, noise
that is estimated with the sources and is non-stationary with glitches (\cref{app:noise}), the cubic
phase of $\ddot f = \tfrac{11}{3}\fdot^2/f_0$, which matters most at the top of the band, and
responses longer than $R$ bins, which the widest sources of the full band now lose
(\cref{app:forward-model-heterodyne}).

Three changes to the training recipe come next. \emph{Slot order.} Slots in order of $f_0$
(\cref{sec:method-chart}) give each slot a fixed share of the window, whether or not a source there
is visible. In order of \ac{snr}, the first slots would hold the visible sources and the last ones
the prior, which suits a varying number of sources, but two sources of similar \ac{snr} can swap
slots between noise draws, and the posterior would again hold both labellings.

\emph{Frame loss.} In the frame's weighted Gaussian likelihood (\cref{eq:fm-frame-loss}), one entry
whose frame collapses far below its error can fill a clipped step on its own
(\cref{app:fm-frame}). A Student-t loss bounds those steps and gave the best full-band model, but
was tried only on the full band with $S=4$.

\emph{Window prior.} Training centres most windows where visible sources are common
(\cref{eq:priors-window}), so the edges of the band see few windows, and the loud sources the flow
misses above about \qty{\ResultsFullSplitMhz}{\milli\hertz} are ones the prior rarely produces
(\cref{sec:results-full}). Moving the weights of \cref{eq:priors-window} towards log-uniform, or a
device per subband, which stratifies every batch over the spectrum, would test whether more
examples close the gap.

% added 2026-11-22 (user): two longer-term extensions, other source classes as streams and the population jointly.
Two extensions reach beyond the \acp{gb}. \emph{Other source classes.} The architecture already
treats sources as one token stream among the data and frame streams
(\cref{sec:method-streams}); massive black-hole binaries, extreme mass-ratio inspirals and other
classes would each enter as a stream of their own, with their own parameter embeddings, attending
to the same data and to each other, so the posterior of one class accounts for the others as a
global fit does. \emph{Population.} The prior is now fixed, fitted once to a simulated catalogue
(\cref{eq:priors-window-prior}). Making its parameters, such as the shape of the Galaxy and the
distribution of $f_0$, $\fdot$ and $\Amp$, random variables drawn per training window, and inferring
them jointly with the sources, would turn the flow into a hierarchical model that learns the
population from the resolved binaries rather than assuming it.
