\subsection{Validating Without the Truth}
\label{sec:validation}

A joint posterior over $8S$ parameters has no closed form, so we score each model in two passes
(\cref{app:evaluation}). The \emph{validation pass} ranks the truth among
\ResultsValidationDraws{} draws for each of \ResultsValidationInjections{} windows simulated from the
prior, in the flow coordinates and in data space (\ac{sbc}, \ac{tarp}, and ranks of the overlap
and the log-likelihood). The \emph{reference pass} compares the flow with joint \ac{mcmc} on
\EvaluationInjections{} evaluation windows (\cref{app:mcmc}): the ratio of the flow's to the chains'
width, $1.4826\,\MAD$ of the draws, and the Jensen--Shannon (JS) divergence of the marginals. A
source is \emph{found} when the flow's $f_0$ width is below one bin, and an $f_0$ draw is
\emph{off-peak} when it lies more than five chain widths and half a bin from the chains' median.

Neither pass alone is sufficient. On mock posteriors with known defects the coordinate ranks accept
a posterior that returns the prior or shuffles parameters between draws, and coverage after
relabelling reads a width floor on loud sources as conservative (\cref{tab:evaluation-power,tab:evaluation-mocks}).
Data-space ranks catch the first two; only a width ratio against a reference shows the third. The
Gaussian mock is not a clean null, so a flow's data-space ranks are read against its offset.

The reference holds only where the chains have converged. For the narrow band with $S=8$ and the
full band with $S=16$ the chains started from Fisher draws never leave that start for faint
sources: below $\snr=\ResultsInvisibleSnr$ their $f_0$ widths are \ResultsNarrowEightFaintMcmcPrior{}
and \ResultsFullSixteenFaintMcmcPrior{} of the prior's, against \ResultsNarrowEightFaintFlowPrior{}
and \ResultsFullSixteenFaintFlowPrior{} for the flow. We therefore compare from $\snr=7$ up, where
prior-started chains confirm the reference for $S=8$; for the full band with $S=16$ they come out
wider, so its ratios up to $\snr\approx20$ are upper bounds on the flow's excess width
(\cref{app:results-mcmc}). The chains serve as a rough reference for the flow's widths, not as a posterior in their own
right, so we do not refine them further.

\subsection{Narrow Band}
\label{sec:results-main}\label{sec:results-narrow}

With two sources per window the flow's posteriors match the joint chains, and the match degrades
as sources are added (\cref{tab:results-summary,fig:results}).

At $\snr=7$--40 and $S=2$ every width ratio lies in
\ResultsNarrowTwoWidthLo--\ResultsNarrowTwoWidthHi, and the off-peak share of loud $f_0$ draws,
\qty{\ResultsNarrowTwoOff}{\percent}, is close to the chains' own \qty{\ResultsNarrowTwoMcmcOff}{\percent}.
With $S=8$ the $f_0$ width is \ResultsNarrowEightFzeroLow{} times the chains' at $\snr=7$--15,
\qty{\ResultsNarrowEightFoundLow}{\percent} of those sources are found and
\qty{\ResultsNarrowEightOff}{\percent} of the loud draws are off-peak. Below $\snr=7$ the flow
returns the prior, as do the chains. The sky is the weakest parameter, with an excess that grows
with the \ac{snr} at every $S$ (\cref{fig:results}b). The JS divergence ranks the models the same
way (\cref{app:results-js}).

\subsection{Full Band}
\label{sec:results-full}

On the full band the flow is close to the chains for faint sources but does not yet resolve the
loud ones. At $\snr=15$--40 its $f_0$ widths are \ResultsFullFourFzeroMid{} to
\ResultsFullSixteenFzeroMid{} times the chains' for $S=4$ to 16; above $\snr=40$ they are
\ResultsFullFourFzeroLoud{} to \ResultsFullSixteenFzeroLoud{} times, and only
\qty{\ResultsFullSixteenFoundLoud}{\percent} of the loud sources are found for $S=16$
(\qty{\ResultsFullFourFoundLoud}{\percent} for $S=4$).

The misses are at high frequency. Above \qty{\ResultsFullSplitMhz}{\milli\hertz} lie
\qty{\ResultsFullSixteenLoudHighShare}{\percent} of the loud sources for $S=16$ but
\qty{\ResultsFullSixteenUnfoundHighShare}{\percent} of those not found, and the excess is mostly the
flow's refinement of its frame: read from the data alone, the frame is wide by design on loud
sources, and the flow narrows it \ResultsFullSixteenContractLow-fold below that frequency but only
\ResultsFullSixteenContractHigh-fold above (\cref{app:results-mcmc}). These sources have the widest
responses, some beyond the $R$ of this configuration (\cref{sec:narrow-band-bands}), and the prior
produces few of them, so training sees few examples\rev{; \cref{sec:followups} proposes how to give it more}.

\subsection{Calibration}
\label{sec:results-calibration}

Coverage is nominal in all six problems, yet the data disagree with every flow slightly. After
relabelling, every model covers \ResultsCoverSixtyEightLo--\ResultsCoverSixtyEightHi{} of the true
coordinates at the \qty{68}{\percent} level, even where the loud full-band $f_0$ widths are several
times the chains': as the mocks predicted, coverage does not see excess width.

The data-space ranks do (\cref{tab:results-validation}, \cref{fig:results-rank-ecdf}). With
\ResultsValidationInjections{} windows the $\gamma$ test \citep{sailynoja2022graphical} rejects even
the Gaussian mock, so we read the offset of the mean rank from one half, positive when the truth
fits the data better than the draws do. Every flow's log-likelihood offset exceeds its mock's by
\ResultsLikeExcessLo{} to \ResultsLikeExcessHi, and its overlap offset is \ResultsFlowOverlapOffsetLo{}
to \ResultsFlowOverlapOffsetHi{} against the mock's \ResultsMockOverlapOffsetLo{} to
\ResultsMockOverlapOffsetHi, the narrow band with $S=2$ included although its marginals have the
chains' widths. No single group of coordinates carries the offset: a draw with a displaced $f_0$
carries a phase that partly makes up for it, so each draw is a coherent fit displaced from the truth
along a direction that mixes the groups (\cref{app:results-validation}). Whether another chart or
loss removes the offset is open.

\rev{\paragraph{By frequency and \acs{snr}.}}\label{sec:results-sbc-heatmap}
Coverage and $p_{\min}$ in \cref{tab:results-summary} pool every source of a problem, but the posterior's
difficulty changes with frequency and loudness. \Cref{fig:sbc-heatmap} splits the \acs{sbc} of each model by
both, each source binned by its draws' median $f_0$ and \acs{snr}, which depend on the data alone: binning by
the true values would condition on the truth, and even exact posteriors would fail. \rev{Of the 100 cells
with enough sources, 14 have $p_{\min}<0.01$, against about 8 expected for exact posteriors, and 3 fall
below the test's resolution of 0.001. They follow no pattern in frequency, \acs{snr} or coordinate, so
binning reveals no region where the flow is miscalibrated beyond the pooled offset above.}
\subsection{Cost of a Posterior}
\label{sec:results-cost}

A posterior costs a fraction of a second. \FlowMatchingCostDraws{} draws of a narrow-band window take
\qty{\FlowMatchingCostNarrow}{\second} on one \ac{gpu} and of a full-band window
\qty{\FlowMatchingCostFull}{\second} (\cref{fig:fm-cost}), with the widths settled by 32 to 64
sampler steps (\cref{app:evaluation-sampler}). A joint \ac{mcmc} chain of one evaluation window takes
\qtyrange{\McmcCostJointLo}{\McmcCostJointHi}{\hour} on \McmcJointCores{} \ac{cpu} cores\rev{ in the narrow band, and about
\qtyrange{\McmcCostFullSixteenLo}{\McmcCostFullSixteenHi}{\hour} in the full band with $S=16$}
(\cref{app:mcmc-cost}).
