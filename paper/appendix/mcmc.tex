% =======================================================================================
\section{Reference \acs{mcmc}}
\label{app:mcmc}
% =======================================================================================

The flow's posteriors are judged against Markov chains that sample the likelihood of
\cref{eq:loglik} directly. The chains are an evaluation reference only: training never sees
them, and the flow's posteriors never use them. This appendix describes the reference sampler,
its convergence, how the Fisher forecast of \cref{app:evaluation-fisher} compares with it, and
what it costs. The flow against these chains is in \cref{app:results}.

\subsection{\acs{mcmc} for Galactic Binaries}
\label{app:mcmc-context}

The standard analysis of \ac{lisa}'s \acp{gb} is a global fit
\citep{cornish2007tests,littenberg2020global,littenberg2023prototype,strub2024global,katz2024efficient,deng2025modular}:
one Markov chain samples all resolvable sources and the noise together, moves between source
counts by reversible jumps \citep{green1995reversible}, and crosses between likelihood modes
(\cref{app:priors}) by parallel tempering \citep{earl2005parallel,vousden2016dynamic}. The band
is split into overlapping segments updated in turn, the unit our windows imitate, and every new
stretch of data or noise model means sampling again. Normalising flows have been proposed as
priors and proposals for these samplers \citep{korsakova2024neural,sasli2026learned}, and
sequential Monte Carlo parallelises their trans-dimensional moves \citep{demasi2026massively}.
Amortised posteriors for one or two binaries have been compared with \ac{mcmc} by
\citet{delmond2026neural}, and slot-based posteriors of sinusoidal mixtures with \ac{rjmcmc} by
\citet{houba2025slotflow} (\cref{app:related}). Our references are simpler than a global fit:
the number of sources and the noise are known, so no move changes the model.

\subsection{Reference Sampler}
\label{app:mcmc-sampler}

Each eval injection (\cref{app:evaluation-injections}) is sampled jointly: one ensemble over all
\McmcJointDim{} parameters of its \McmcSlots{} sources, on the window likelihood of
\cref{eq:loglik}. Each source's response (\McmcResponsePoints{} bins in the narrow band) is added
into the window, bins outside it dropped as in the simulator, so one likelihood costs one response
per source. The chains sample the joint posterior $p(\theta\mid d)$, whose per-source marginals are
the flow's target; no source is held at its true value.

\paragraph{Coordinates and prior.}
Each source is sampled in $u = (\ln f_0, \ln\fdot, \ln\Amp, \alpha, \delta, \psi, \cos\iota,
\phi_0)$ under the training prior of \cref{app:priors}, independently across sources: the Gaussian
in $(\log_{10}f_0, \log_{10}\fdot, \log_{10}\Amp)$ of \cref{app:priors-sources} with $f_0$ inside
the window, $\cos\iota$, $\psi$ and $\phi_0$ uniform, and $(\alpha,\delta)$ with the Galactic sky
density times $\cos\delta$. The chains add one cut, $\log_{10}\fdot$ at most \McmcFdotSigmas{}
standard deviations above its mean given $f_0$, which removes a prior mass of
\num{\McmcFdotCutMass}. The sampler moves in a box: $\alpha$, $\psi$ and $\phi_0$ on full $2\pi$
periods centred on the truth, so no mode near it is cut by an edge, and $\ln\fdot$ and $\ln\Amp$
out to \McmcBoxSigmas{} conditional standard deviations from their prior mean anywhere in the
window, short of the $\fdot$ cut. The likelihood is computed in double precision.

\paragraph{Moves, start and length.}
The sampler is a parallel-tempered ensemble Metropolis--Hastings sampler from the \ac{lisa}
community; each iteration is a temperature swap followed, with equal probability, by a
differential-evolution move \citep{terbraak2006markov} or an affine-invariant stretch move
\citep{goodman2010ensemble,foreman2013emcee}, with \McmcTemps{} temperatures spaced geometrically
from the cold chain to \McmcTempMax{} \citep{earl2005parallel,vousden2016dynamic}. Tempering lets
a chain leave the mode it starts in. The walkers start from the injection's Fisher draws
(\cref{app:evaluation-fisher}), with $\fdot$ redrawn from its prior at each draw's $f_0$, clipped
into the box and under the $\fdot$ cut. Starting near the truth suits a reference, not inference:
it skips the search, and burn-in and tempering remove the start. Each of the \McmcInjections{}
eval injections runs \McmcSeeds{} seeds, \McmcChains{} chains in all
(\cref{tab:mcmc-settings}); their cold draws, pooled over the seeds, are subsampled to
\McmcKeepDraws{} per injection for the evaluation.

\subsection{Reference Posteriors}
\label{app:mcmc-posteriors}

Before any width is taken, the draws are relabelled to the injected sources by
\cref{eq:evaluation-matching}, folded onto the injection's branch of the exact $(\psi,\phi_0)$
symmetries of \cref{eq:psi-phi-symmetry} (the likelihood is checked to be invariant on the
draws), and $f_0$ is expressed in bins. Every width in this appendix is the robust
$1.4826\,\MAD$ of the folded draws, per parameter.

\Cref{fig:mcmc-posterior} shows what the reference looks like for one injection. For the louder
sources the posterior is close to Gaussian, and the Fisher forecast follows its
correlations: amplitude against inclination, and $\psi$ against $\phi_0$. The faintest source
is not. It puts \qty{\McmcPosteriorFarPercent}{\percent} of its draws in a second frequency mode
\McmcPosteriorFarBins{} bins away, and its declination is shifted by a median
\qty{\McmcPosteriorDecShift}{\radian} from the truth; the unimodal forecast, centred on the truth, sees
neither.

\subsection{Convergence}
\label{app:mcmc-convergence}

For each parameter we compute the split $\hat R$ of \citet{gelman1992inference} over
\McmcRhatGroups{} groups of cold walkers and the integrated autocorrelation time $\tau$, with
emcee's estimator on the walker-averaged autocorrelation and Sokal's window
\citep{foreman2013emcee}; the \ac{ess} is walkers $\times$ kept iterations$/\tau$. Over the
\McmcJointChains{} chains, $\hat R\le\McmcJointRhatMax$, $\tau\le\McmcJointTauMax$ and
$\acs{ess}\ge\McmcJointEssMin$; the seeds' widths agree within
\qty{\McmcJointSeedWidthTypical}{\percent} for \qty{\McmcJointSeedShare}{\percent} of them and within
\qty{\McmcJointSeedWidthDev}{\percent} for all, the largest gap being the $f_0$ width of the faintest
source. Since $\tau$ comes out at about one thinning
interval, it is an upper bound and the \ac{ess} a lower one.

\paragraph{Burn-in and length.}
Agreement between walkers can hide a chain that has not settled. The sampler is therefore
rerun unchanged on injections \McmcConvRuns{} for \McmcConvIterations{} iterations with
\McmcConvSeeds{} seeds each, storing every \McmcConvThin{}th iteration from the start. Each
marginal's drift is measured against the other seeds' second halves, so a seed stuck in its own
mode shows up: the shift of the median in half \qty{68}{\percent} widths, and the relative change
of the \qty{68}{\percent} width. A burn-in $b$ passes when $b\ge\McmcConvBurnTaus\,\tau_{\max}$
and every segment of \McmcConvStep{} iterations from $b$ on keeps the median within
\McmcConvMedianTol{} half-widths and the width within \qty{\McmcConvWidthTol}{\percent}. A kept
length passes when every parameter has $\acs{ess}\ge\McmcConvEssCrit$ per seed (enough to resolve
a width to a few per cent), $\hat R<\McmcConvRhatCrit$ across seeds, and the same drift
tolerances.

\Cref{fig:mcmc-convergence} shows the result. At the paper's setting the kept draws pass on every
injection: $\tau_{\max}$ is \McmcConvTauLo--\McmcConvTauHi{} iterations, the \ac{ess} at least
\McmcConvEssLo{} per seed, $\hat R\le\McmcConvRhatHi$, and the pooled drift at most
\McmcConvDriftMedianHi{} in the median and \McmcConvDriftWidthHi{} in the width. The strict
burn-in test holds only on injections \McmcConvPassing, which settle after
\McmcConvPassingBurns{} iterations. Of injections \McmcConvFailing, the first fails narrowly, its
worst later segment at \McmcConvMildAfter{} times the tolerance in \McmcConvMildParam. The second
never settles: in one seed \McmcConvWorstParam{} (\ac{snr} \McmcConvWorstSnr) hops between sky
modes, from \McmcConvWorstFirst{} times the tolerance in the first segment to
\McmcConvWorstAfter{} times it after the burn-in. Pooled over the kept length and the seeds these
excursions average out, so that injection's reference for that parameter is a mixture of modes
with unpinned weights: fair for widths, weak for the shape of that marginal.

\subsection{Fisher against \acs{mcmc}}
\label{app:mcmc-fisher}

\begin{figure}[!htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics{figures/mcmc.pdf}
  \caption{Fisher widths over the joint \ac{mcmc} widths, per source of the
  \McmcJointWindows{} injections with chains, against source \ac{snr}. Lines span the seven
  parameters other than the drift coordinate (\McmcDrift{} in these chains), dots mark their
  median, diamonds mark the drift; the shaded band is \McmcFisherLo--\McmcFisherHi. Loud sources
  sit in the band.}
  \label{fig:mcmc-widths}
\end{figure}

The Fisher forecast is the cheap reference used on every injection; the chains test it
(\cref{fig:mcmc-widths}). For the \McmcFisherHighCount{} sources with \ac{snr} at least
\McmcFisherHighSnr, \McmcFisherInside{} of the \McmcFisherTotal{} width ratios outside the drift
lie in \McmcFisherLo--\McmcFisherHi. The others are \McmcFisherOutliers; the $\alpha$ outlier is
the mode-hopping source of \cref{app:mcmc-convergence}, of which the Fisher matrix sees one mode.
For the \McmcFisherLowCount{} sources below \ac{snr} \McmcFisherLowSnr{} the ratios spread over
\McmcFisherLowLo--\McmcFisherLowHi: near the \ac{snr} floor the likelihood is far from Gaussian.
The \ac{mcmc} width of the drift coordinate is \McmcDriftWidthLo--\McmcDriftWidthHi{} for every
source, whatever its \ac{snr}: the drift is not measured in these windows, and the forecast's
Gaussian stand-in for its prior gives \McmcFisherDriftLo--\McmcFisherDriftHi{} of the \ac{mcmc}
width. The forecast is therefore a fair proxy for the widths of loud sources outside the drift,
and no proxy for faint sources or the drift.

\subsection{Independent Check}
\label{app:mcmc-strub}

The sampler and likelihood are also run on a published analysis, the faint verification binary
of \citet[Table~VII]{strub2022bayesian}, under its authors' priors. They reproduce its posterior
and its \ac{snr} gain from the observed to the maximum-likelihood value (\cref{app:noise},
\cref{tab:noise-strub,fig:strub-source}).

\subsection{Cost}
\label{app:mcmc-cost}

On \McmcJointCores{} CPU cores, a chain takes \qtyrange{\McmcCostJointLo}{\McmcCostJointHi}{\hour}
per injection and seed, against a fraction of a second for the flow (\cref{app:fm-sampling}). The
chains also scale with the band, a full-band response being \McmcResponsePointsFull{} bins against
\McmcResponsePoints. This cost is why the full-band chains are still missing.
