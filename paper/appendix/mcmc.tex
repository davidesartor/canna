% =======================================================================================
\section{Reference \acs{mcmc}}
\label{app:mcmc}
% =======================================================================================

The flow's posteriors are judged against Markov chains that sample the likelihood of
\cref{eq:loglik} directly. The chains are an evaluation reference only: training never sees
them, and the flow's posteriors never use them. This appendix describes the two samplers, their
convergence, how they compare with each other and with the Fisher forecast of
\cref{app:evaluation-fisher}, and what they cost. The flow against these chains is in
\cref{app:results}.

\subsection{\acs{mcmc} for Galactic Binaries}
\label{app:mcmc-context}

The standard analysis of \ac{lisa}'s \acp{gb} is a global fit
\citep{cornish2007tests,littenberg2020global,littenberg2023prototype,strub2024global,katz2024efficient,deng2025modular}:
one Markov chain samples all resolvable sources and the noise together, moves between source
counts by reversible jumps \citep{green1995reversible}, and crosses between likelihood modes
(\cref{app:priors}) by parallel tempering \citep{earl2005parallel,vousden2016dynamic}. The band
is split into overlapping segments updated in turn, the unit our windows imitate, and every new
stretch of data or noise model means sampling again. Normalising flows have been proposed as
priors and proposals for these samplers \citep{korsakova2024neural,sasli2026learned}, and
sequential Monte Carlo parallelises their trans-dimensional moves \citep{demasi2026massively}.
Amortised posteriors for one or two binaries have been compared with \ac{mcmc} by
\citet{delmond2026neural}, and slot-based posteriors of sinusoidal mixtures with \ac{rjmcmc} by
\citet{houba2025slotflow} (\cref{app:related}). Our references are simpler than a global fit:
the number of sources and the noise are known, so no move changes the model.

\subsection{Narrowband Sampler}
\label{app:mcmc-narrow}

Each source $s$ of each eval injection (\cref{app:evaluation-injections}) is sampled alone, in
its eight parameters, with the other sources held at their true values $\theta^\star_{s'}$.
With the residual $\tilde r = \tilde d - \sum_{s'\neq s}\tilde h(\theta^\star_{s'})$,
\cref{eq:loglik} becomes, up to a constant,
\begin{equation}
  \log p(r\mid\theta_s) = -\sum_{c}\sum_{k\in K(\theta_s)}
  \frac{\bigl|\tilde r_{k,c}-\tilde h_{k,c}(\theta_s)\bigr|^2-\bigl|\tilde r_{k,c}\bigr|^2}{P_c(f_k)},
  \label{eq:mcmc-narrow}
\end{equation}
where $K(\theta_s)$ are the bins of the source's response (\McmcResponsePoints{} in the narrow
band) inside the window. Outside $K$ the source contributes nothing, and the $|\tilde r|^2$ term
makes the sum over $K$ differ from the full-window sum by a constant, so \cref{eq:mcmc-narrow} is
\cref{eq:loglik} with the other sources fixed, at the cost of one response. Its posterior is the
conditional $p(\theta_s\mid d,\theta^\star_{-s})$: the marginal of the joint posterior when the
source overlaps no other, narrower next to a neighbour whose uncertainty it removes
(\cref{app:mcmc-agreement}).

\paragraph{Coordinates and prior.}
Each source is sampled in $u = (\ln f_0, \ln\Mc, \ln\Amp, \alpha, \delta, \psi, \cos\iota,
\phi_0)$ under the training prior of \cref{app:priors}. In these coordinates $\ln f_0$ is uniform
over the window, $\cos\iota$, $\psi$ and $\phi_0$ are uniform, and $(\alpha,\delta)$ has the
Galactic sky density times $\cos\delta$. The chirp-mass density has no closed form; it is
computed by quadrature over the primary mass and interpolated, and matches
\McmcMcCheckDraws{} prior draws with $\chi^2/\text{dof} = \McmcMcCheckChi$ over
\McmcMcCheckBins{} bins. The amplitude prior is log-uniform in the source's own \ac{snr}
$\snr_s = \inner{h(\theta_s)}{h(\theta_s)}^{1/2}$ on $[\McmcSnrMin,\McmcSnrMax]$; since
$\snr_s\propto\Amp$ at fixed other parameters, it is flat in $\ln\Amp$ with the \ac{snr} range
as a cut, applied in the likelihood. The sampler moves in a box: $\alpha$, $\psi$ and $\phi_0$ on
full $2\pi$ periods centred on the truth, so no mode near it is cut by an edge, and $\ln\Amp$
within $\ln(\McmcSnrMax/\McmcSnrMin)$ of its true value. The likelihood is computed in double
precision.

\paragraph{Moves, start and length.}
The sampler is a parallel-tempered ensemble Metropolis--Hastings sampler from the \ac{lisa}
community; each iteration is a temperature swap followed, with equal probability, by a
differential-evolution move \citep{terbraak2006markov} or an affine-invariant stretch move
\citep{goodman2010ensemble,foreman2013emcee}, with temperatures spaced geometrically from the
cold chain to \McmcTempMax{} \citep{earl2005parallel,vousden2016dynamic}. Tempering lets a chain
leave the mode it starts in. The walkers start from a Gaussian about the truth with the inverse
Fisher covariance of \cref{eq:mcmc-narrow}, each box added as a weak Gaussian to keep it finite,
with chirp masses replaced by prior draws (\cref{app:mcmc-fisher}) and amplitudes rescaled into
the \ac{snr} range. Starting near the truth suits a reference, not inference: it skips the
search, and burn-in and tempering remove the start. \Cref{tab:mcmc-settings} lists the run
lengths. With \McmcSlots{} sources in each of the \McmcWindows{} eval windows (window $j$ is the
injection at the $j$-th \ac{snr} quantile) and \McmcSeeds{} seeds, the narrow band has
\McmcSources{} sources and \McmcChains{} chains.

\subsection{Joint Sampler}
\label{app:mcmc-joint}

To see what conditioning on the true neighbours does, \McmcJointWindows{} narrow-band windows,
with window \acp{snr} \McmcJointSnrLo--\McmcJointSnrHi{} and source \acp{snr}
\McmcJointSrcSnrLo--\McmcJointSrcSnrHi, are also sampled jointly: one ensemble over all
\McmcJointDim{} parameters on the full-window likelihood, each source with the coordinates and
prior above (\cref{tab:mcmc-settings}). The walkers start from the window's Fisher draws, with
chirp masses from the prior.

Before any width is taken, the joint draws are relabelled to the injected sources by
\cref{eq:evaluation-matching}, folded onto the injection's branch of the exact $(\psi,\phi_0)$
symmetries of \cref{eq:psi-phi-symmetry} (the likelihood is checked to be invariant on the
draws), and $f_0$ is expressed in bins; the narrowband draws are folded the same way. Every width
in this appendix is the robust $1.4826\,\MAD$ of the folded draws, per parameter.

\Cref{fig:mcmc-posterior} shows what the reference looks like for one window. For the louder
sources the posterior is close to Gaussian, and the Fisher forecast follows its
correlations: amplitude against inclination, and $\psi$ against $\phi_0$. The faintest source
is not. It puts \qty{\McmcPosteriorFarPercent}{\percent} of its draws in a second frequency mode
\McmcPosteriorFarBins{} bins away, and its declination is shifted by a median
\qty{\McmcPosteriorDecShift}{\radian} from the truth; the unimodal forecast, centred on the truth, sees
neither.

\subsection{Convergence}
\label{app:mcmc-convergence}

For each parameter we compute the split $\hat R$ of \citet{gelman1992inference} over
\McmcRhatGroups{} groups of cold walkers and the integrated autocorrelation time $\tau$, with
emcee's estimator on the walker-averaged autocorrelation and Sokal's window
\citep{foreman2013emcee}; the \ac{ess} is walkers $\times$ kept iterations$/\tau$. Over all
\McmcChains{} narrowband chains, $\hat R\le\McmcRhatMax$, $\tau\le\McmcTauMax$ iterations and
$\acs{ess}\ge\McmcEssMin$, and the two seeds' widths agree within \qty{\McmcSeedWidthDev}{\percent}. Over the
\McmcJointChains{} joint chains, $\hat R\le\McmcJointRhatMax$, $\tau\le\McmcJointTauMax$ and
$\acs{ess}\ge\McmcJointEssMin$; the seeds agree within \qty{\McmcJointSeedWidthDev}{\percent}, the largest gap
being the $f_0$ width of the faintest source. Since $\tau$ comes out at about one thinning
interval, it is an upper bound and the \ac{ess} a lower one.

\paragraph{Burn-in and length.}
Agreement between walkers can hide a chain that has not settled. The joint sampler is therefore
rerun unchanged for \McmcConvIterations{} iterations with \McmcConvSeeds{} seeds per window,
storing every \McmcConvThin{}th iteration from the start. Each marginal's drift is measured
against the other seeds' second halves, so a seed stuck in its own mode shows up: the shift of
the median in half \qty{68}{\percent} widths, and the relative change of the \qty{68}{\percent} width. A burn-in $b$ passes
when $b\ge\McmcConvBurnTaus\,\tau_{\max}$ and every segment of \McmcConvStep{} iterations from
$b$ on keeps the median within \McmcConvMedianTol{} half-widths and the width within
\qty{\McmcConvWidthTol}{\percent}. A kept length passes when every parameter has $\acs{ess}\ge\McmcConvEssCrit$
per seed (enough to resolve a width to a few per cent), $\hat R<\McmcConvRhatCrit$ across seeds,
and the same drift tolerances.

\Cref{fig:mcmc-convergence} shows the result. At the paper's setting the kept draws pass in every
window: $\tau_{\max}$ is \McmcConvTauLo--\McmcConvTauHi{} iterations, the \ac{ess} at least
\McmcConvEssLo{} per seed, $\hat R\le\McmcConvRhatHi$, and the pooled drift at most
\McmcConvDriftMedianHi{} in the median and \McmcConvDriftWidthHi{} in the width. The strict
burn-in test holds only in windows \McmcConvPassing, which settle after \McmcConvBurnNine{} and
\McmcConvBurnSeven{} iterations. Of windows \McmcConvFailing, the first fails narrowly, its worst later segment at
\McmcConvWindowFiveAfter{} times the tolerance in \McmcConvWindowFiveParam. The second never
settles: in one seed the right ascension of its loudest source (\ac{snr} \McmcConvWindowTwoSnr)
hops between sky modes, from \McmcConvWindowTwoFirst{} times the tolerance in the first segment
to \McmcConvWindowTwoAfter{} times it after the burn-in. Pooled over the kept length and the
seeds these excursions average out, so that window's reference for that $\alpha$ is a mixture of
modes with unpinned weights: fair for widths, weak for the shape of that marginal.

\subsection{Narrowband against Joint}
\label{app:mcmc-agreement}

\Cref{fig:mcmc-widths}b compares the narrowband widths with the joint ones over the
\McmcJointSources{} sources of the joint windows. For a source whose response overlaps no other,
the two must agree; where responses overlap, the narrowband posterior should be narrower, since
it holds the neighbours at the truth, and for a Gaussian a conditional variance never exceeds the
marginal one. The data follow this pattern: of the \McmcNarrowJointCount{} width ratios outside
$\ln\Mc$, \McmcNarrowJointNear{} lie within \qty{\McmcNarrowJointTol}{\percent} of one, \McmcNarrowJointBelow{}
below and \McmcNarrowJointAbove{} above; their median is \McmcNarrowJointMedian, and
\qty{\McmcNarrowJointWithin}{\percent} lie in \McmcNarrowJointBandLo--\McmcNarrowJointBandHi. The
ratios outside that band sit in \McmcNarrowJointWideSources{} sources. The ratio spans
\McmcNarrowJointLo--\McmcNarrowJointHi, both extremes from the faintest source (\ac{snr}
\McmcNarrowJointLoSnr), where neither posterior is Gaussian; the next largest gap is
\McmcNarrowJointNextParam{} of the \ac{snr}-\McmcNarrowJointNextSnr{} source of window
\McmcNarrowJointNextWindow{} (\McmcNarrowJointNextRatio), whose joint chain hops between sky modes
(\cref{app:mcmc-convergence}). In $\ln\Mc$, whose width is the prior's, the ratio is
\McmcNarrowJointMcLo--\McmcNarrowJointMcHi. The narrowband chains are thus a fair reference for
the flow's target, the marginal of the joint posterior, except where neighbours overlap strongly.

\subsection{Fisher against \acs{mcmc}}
\label{app:mcmc-fisher}

\begin{figure}[!htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/mcmc.pdf}
  \caption{Posterior widths over the joint \ac{mcmc} widths, per source of the
  \McmcJointWindows{} joint windows, against source \ac{snr}. (a) The Fisher forecast. (b) The
  narrowband \ac{mcmc}. Lines span the seven parameters other than $\ln\Mc$, dots mark their
  median, diamonds mark $\ln\Mc$; the shaded band is \McmcFisherLo--\McmcFisherHi. Loud sources
  sit in the band in (a); in (b) ratios fall below one where neighbours overlap.}
  \label{fig:mcmc-widths}
\end{figure}

The Fisher forecast is the cheap reference used on every injection; the joint chains test it
(\cref{fig:mcmc-widths}a). For the \McmcFisherHighCount{} sources with \ac{snr} at least
\McmcFisherHighSnr, \McmcFisherInside{} of the \McmcFisherTotal{} width ratios outside $\ln\Mc$
lie in \McmcFisherLo--\McmcFisherHi. The others are \McmcFisherOutliers; the $\alpha$ outlier is
the mode-hopping source of \cref{app:mcmc-convergence}, of which the Fisher matrix sees one mode.
Against the narrowband chains of all \McmcWindows{} windows,
\qty{\McmcFisherNarrowInside}{\percent} of the ratios outside $\ln\Mc$ of the
\McmcFisherNarrowSources{} sources above \ac{snr} \McmcFisherLowSnr{} lie in the band; the
forecast misses by more than a factor of two for \McmcFisherNarrowMisses{} sources at \ac{snr}
\McmcFisherNarrowMissSnrLo--\McmcFisherNarrowMissSnrHi, by up to \McmcFisherNarrowMissMax, along
the $\ln\Amp$--$\cos\iota$ and $\psi$--$\phi_0$ degeneracies. For the
\McmcFisherLowCount{} joint-window sources below \ac{snr} \McmcFisherLowSnr{} the ratios spread over
\McmcFisherLowLo--\McmcFisherLowHi: near the \ac{snr} floor the likelihood is far from Gaussian
and the \ac{snr} cut truncates it. The \ac{mcmc} width of $\ln\Mc$ is
\McmcMcWidthLo--\McmcMcWidthHi{} for every source, the robust width of the prior itself
(\McmcMcPriorWidth): the chirp mass is not measured in these windows. The forecast's Gaussian
stand-in for that prior gives \McmcFisherMcLo--\McmcFisherMcHi{} of the \ac{mcmc} width. The
forecast is therefore a fair proxy for the widths of loud sources outside $\ln\Mc$, and no proxy
for faint sources or the chirp mass.

\subsection{Independent Check}
\label{app:mcmc-strub}

The sampler and likelihood are also run on a published analysis, the faint verification binary
of \citet[Table~VII]{strub2022bayesian}, under its authors' priors. They reproduce its posterior
and its \ac{snr} gain from the observed to the maximum-likelihood value (\cref{app:noise},
\cref{tab:noise-strub,fig:strub-source}).

\subsection{Cost}
\label{app:mcmc-cost}

On \McmcCores{} CPU cores, a narrowband chain takes \qtyrange{\McmcCostSourceLo}{\McmcCostSourceHi}{\minute}
per source and seed, \qtyrange{\McmcCostWindowLo}{\McmcCostWindowHi}{\minute} for the
\McmcSlots{} sources of a window; a joint chain on \McmcJointCores{} cores takes
\qtyrange{\McmcCostJointLo}{\McmcCostJointHi}{\hour} per window and seed, against a fraction of a
second for the flow (\cref{app:fm-sampling}). The chains also scale with the band, a full-band response being
\McmcResponsePointsFull{} bins against \McmcResponsePoints. This cost is why the joint sampler
covers only \McmcJointWindows{} windows and the full-band chains are still missing.
