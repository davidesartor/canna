\section{Priors and the Training Distribution}
\label{app:priors}
% =======================================================================================

This appendix states what the flow is trained on, which \cref{sec:method} uses: the per-source prior,
the prior over windows, the number of sources a window holds, and the stream of simulated windows.
\Cref{tab:priors-bands} collects what differs between the two bands; $S={}$\ResultsNarrowCounts{} on
the narrow band and \ResultsFullCounts{} on the full band in the runs of \cref{app:results}.

\subsection{The Sources of a Window}
\label{app:priors-sources}

The $S$ sources of a window are independent draws from the per-source prior of
\cref{tab:priors-source}, so they are exchangeable. Only $f_0$ depends on the window directly. For
frequency, drift and amplitude we fit one Gaussian by maximum likelihood to the logarithms of the
binaries above \qty{\PriorsCatalogueCutMhz}{\milli\hertz} of a simulated catalogue of double white
dwarfs \citep{perego2025towards,breivik2020cosmic},
\begin{equation}
  \begin{pmatrix}\log_{10}f_0\\ \log_{10}\fdot\\ \log_{10}\Amp\end{pmatrix}
  \sim \Normal(\mu_\pi, \Sigma_\pi),
  \label{eq:priors-source}
\end{equation}
with $f_0$ truncated to the window (\cref{eq:priors-interior}), and, with $f_0$ in \unit{\hertz} and
$\fdot$ in \unit{\hertz\per\second},
\begin{equation}
  \mu_\pi = \begin{pmatrix}\PriorsMeanF\\ \PriorsMeanFdot\\ \PriorsMeanAmp\end{pmatrix},\qquad
  \Sigma_\pi = \begin{pmatrix}
    \PriorsCovFF & \PriorsCovFFdot & \PriorsCovFAmp\\
    \PriorsCovFFdot & \PriorsCovFdotFdot & \PriorsCovFdotAmp\\
    \PriorsCovFAmp & \PriorsCovFdotAmp & \PriorsCovAmpAmp
  \end{pmatrix}.
  \label{eq:priors-mean}
\end{equation}
Other population models of the Galaxy's double white dwarfs
\citep{toonen2012supernova,korol2020populations,georgousi2022gravitational,thiele2023applying}
differ in their binary physics \citep{toubiana2024interacting,boileau2025gravitational}; refitting
changes only $\mu_\pi$ and $\Sigma_\pi$. Most sources barely drift: the full band's median drift over
the observation is \PriorsFullDriftMedian{} of a bin, and \qty{\PriorsFullDriftOver}{\percent} of its
sources drift by more than one. The sky is a mixture of a bulge, a thick and a thin disc and an
isotropic floor in galactic longitude $l$ and $\sin b$,
\begin{equation}
  \pi(l, \sin b) = w_{\rm b}\,\Normal(l; 0, \sigma_l^2)\,\Normal(\sin b; 0, \sigma_b^2)
  + \sum_{d\in\{\rm thick, thin\}} w_d\,\mathcal{L}_{\pi}(l; \lambda_{l,d})\,\mathcal{L}_{1}(\sin b; \lambda_{b,d})
  + \frac{w_{\rm f}}{4\pi},
  \label{eq:priors-sky}
\end{equation}
with $\mathcal{L}_c(x;\lambda)\propto e^{-|x|/\lambda}$ a Laplace density truncated to $|x|\le c$,
rotated to the equatorial $(\alpha,\delta)$ of the response. It puts \qty{\PriorsBulgeWeight}{\percent}
of its weight in the bulge, \qty{\PriorsThickWeight}{\percent} and \qty{\PriorsThinWeight}{\percent} in
the thick and thin discs and \qty{\PriorsFloorWeight}{\percent} in the floor, and traces the plane:
\qty{\PriorsPlaneShare}{\percent} of the sources lie within \ang{\PriorsPlaneDeg} of the galactic
plane (\cref{fig:priors}b).

\subsection{The Window Prior}
\label{app:priors-window}

A window is $4R$ consecutive bins starting at an even bin $k_0 = \PriorsStride{}j$, with centre
$\fw = (k_0 + 2R)/\Tobs$ and interior
\begin{equation}
  f_0\Tobs \in \bigl[\max(k_0 + R/2,\ f_{\min}\Tobs),\ \min(k_0 + 4R - R/2,\ f_{\max}\Tobs)\bigr]
  \label{eq:priors-interior}
\end{equation}
(\cref{app:forward-model-windows}). Windows centred on the catalogue's $f_0$ marginal would sit low in
the band, where sources are faint: the upper half of the full band in $\log f_0$, which holds most of
the visible sources, would get \qty{\PriorsFullTopShare}{\percent} of the windows, and
\qty{\PriorsFullWithVisible}{\percent} of windows would hold a visible source. Training therefore
centres windows where sources can be seen. Of \PriorsCandidates{} candidates $\log_{10}f_c^{(m)}$
drawn uniformly over the band, each with one prior sky and orientation and alone in its window at the
conditional mean $\fdot$, one is picked with weight
\begin{equation}
\begin{gathered}
  w_m \propto \Normal\bigl(\log_{10}f_c^{(m)};\, \PriorsMeanF, \PriorsStdF^2\bigr)\,
  \Phi\!\Bigl(\frac{\PriorsInterceptAmp + \PriorsSlopeAmp\log_{10}f_c^{(m)} - \log_{10}\Amp_k^{(m)}}{\PriorsResidualAmp}\Bigr),\\
  j = \operatorname{clip}\!\Bigl(\operatorname{round}\frac{f_c\Tobs - 2R}{\PriorsStride{}},\ j_{\rm lo},\ j_{\rm hi}\Bigr),
\end{gathered}
  \label{eq:priors-window}
\end{equation}
the catalogue's $f_0$ marginal times the chance that $\log_{10}\Amp$ exceeds $\log_{10}\Amp_k^{(m)}$,
the amplitude at which the candidate reaches $\snr=k=\PriorsVisibleSnr$; $j_{\rm lo}$ and
$j_{\rm hi}$ are the first and last windows whose interior reaches the band. A share of
\qty{\PriorsUniformWindowsPercent}{\percent} of windows skips the weights and is centred
log-uniformly, so training also sees the empty windows that most of the band holds. This puts
\qty{\PriorsFullTrainingTopShare}{\percent} of the full band's windows in its upper half and
\qty{\PriorsFullTrainingWithVisible}{\percent} with a visible source; in the narrow band the share
rises from \qty{\PriorsNarrowWithVisible}{\percent} to \qty{\PriorsNarrowTrainingWithVisible}{\percent}.
Given the window, the prior is the catalogue's; the weights only choose which windows training sees,
so they do not change the posterior the flow learns.

\subsection{Sources per Window}
\label{app:priors-counts}

A window holds each of the \num{\RatesCatalogue} sources of the simulated Galaxy with the prior's share
of $f_0$ in it, so its count is binomial (\cref{eq:gw-rates}); the visible sources follow the same law
thinned by $P(\snr\ge\RatesVisibleSnr\mid f_0)$, with the amplitude integrated out in closed form.
\Cref{tab:narrow-band-bands} gives the counts in each subband. A window of the full run's
\RatesFullWindow{} bins holds up to \num{\RatesFullAllHi} sources and \RatesFullVisibleMax{} visible
ones, near \qty{\RatesFullBusiestMhz}{\milli\hertz}, and one of the narrow run's \RatesNarrowWindow{}
bins \numrange{\RatesNarrowAllLo}{\RatesNarrowAllHi} sources and
\numrange{\RatesNarrowVisibleLo}{\RatesNarrowVisibleMax} visible ones. The $S\le16$ slots of our runs
are therefore far below the source count of a real window at a few millihertz: they test how a joint
amortised posterior scales with the number of overlapping sources, not a complete model of the
Galactic foreground.

\subsection{The Training Distribution}
\label{app:priors-training}

There is no fixed dataset: every example is a freshly simulated window, with a centre $\fw$ by
\cref{eq:priors-window}, sources $\theta_{1:S}\sim\pi_w$, noisy data in the window's $4R$ bins whitened
to the conditioning input $y$ (\cref{app:noise}), and the target $X_1 = \varphi(\theta_{1:S};\fw)$ in
flow coordinates, $S\times\PriorsCoords{}$, sources in order of $f_0$ (\cref{eq:chart}). Each window
is paired with \PriorsDraws{} standard normal base points $Z_0$ with their own flow times $t$
(\cref{eq:loss}, \cref{app:fm}), drawn inside the compiled training step, so a batch of
\num{\PriorsBatch} pairs is $\PriorsObservationsPerStep{}$ windows. A run sees
$\num{\PriorsFullObservations}$ to $\num{\PriorsNarrowObservations}$ independent windows
(\cref{tab:priors-bands}), so overfitting is not a concern and comparisons between runs are
comparisons of optimisation. The simulation takes \qty{\PriorsNarrowDrawShare}{\percent} of a step in
the narrow band and \qty{\PriorsFullDrawShare}{\percent} in the full band.
