The noise of \cref{eq:noise-data} is the instrument noise of the \ac{lisa} requirements
\citep{lisasst2018lisa}, in the single-link form of \citet[Issue~1.1]{babak2021lisa}: an optical
metrology noise and a test-mass acceleration noise,
\begin{align}
  P_{\rm oms}(f) &= \bigl(\qty{\NoiseOms}{\pico\metre}\bigr)^2
  \left[1+\Bigl(\frac{\qty{2}{\milli\hertz}}{f}\Bigr)^4\right]\unit{\per\hertz}, \nonumber\\
  P_{\rm acc}(f) &= \bigl(\qty{\NoiseAcc}{\femto\metre\per\second\squared}\bigr)^2
  \left[1+\Bigl(\frac{\qty{0.4}{\milli\hertz}}{f}\Bigr)^2\right]
  \left[1+\Bigl(\frac{f}{\qty{8}{\milli\hertz}}\Bigr)^4\right]\unit{\per\hertz},
  \label{eq:noise-single-link}
\end{align}
converted to fractional frequency by $S_{\rm oms} = (2\pi f/c)^2P_{\rm oms}$ and
$S_{\rm acc} = P_{\rm acc}/[(2\pi f)^2c^2]$. For equal arms, $x = 2\pi fL$, the \acs{tdi}-1.5 channels
have the one-sided \acp{psd} \citep[eqs.~19, 127--129]{babak2021lisa}
\begin{align}
  S_A = S_E &= 8\sin^2x\,\bigl[(2+\cos x)\,S_{\rm oms}
  + 4\,(1 + \cos x + \cos^2 x)\,S_{\rm acc}\bigr], \nonumber\\
  S_T &= 16\sin^2x\,(1-\cos x)\,\bigl[S_{\rm oms} + 2\,(1-\cos x)\,S_{\rm acc}\bigr].
  \label{eq:noise-psd}
\end{align}
The channel $T$ is quiet ($S_T/S_A = \num{\NoiseTOverALow}$ at \qty{\NoiseBandLow}{\milli\hertz}) but
carries almost no \ac{snr} in our band. The noise is instrumental only: no Galactic foreground
\citep{karnesis2021characterization}, gaps or glitches. Signals are compared through
\begin{equation}
  \inner{a}{b} = 4\,\mathrm{Re}\sum_{c}\sum_{k}\frac{a_{k,c}\,b^*_{k,c}}{S_c(f_k)\,\Tobs},
  \label{eq:inner}
\end{equation}
so that the log-likelihood of \cref{eq:loglik} is $-\tfrac12\inner{d-h}{d-h}$ up to a constant and
$\snr_s = \inner{h(\theta_s)}{h(\theta_s)}^{1/2}$. The simulator matches independent references: over
\num{\NoiseGeneratorWindows} pure-noise windows $\inner{n}{n}$ follows $\chi^2_{\NoiseGeneratorDof{}}$
(Kolmogorov--Smirnov $p = \NoiseGeneratorKs{}$), and for the verification binary of
\citet[Table~VII]{strub2022bayesian} our \acs{snr} is \NoiseStrubSnr{} against their
\NoiseStrubSnrTheirs{}.
