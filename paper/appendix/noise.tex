The noise is the instrument noise of the \ac{lisa} science requirements \citep{lisasst2018lisa}, in
the single-link form of \citet[Issue~1.1]{babak2021lisa}: an \ac{oms} displacement noise and a
test-mass acceleration noise on each link (their eqs.~9 and 11),
\begin{align}
  P_{\rm oms}(f) &= \bigl(\qty{\NoiseOms}{\pico\metre}\bigr)^2
  \left[1+\Bigl(\frac{\qty{2}{\milli\hertz}}{f}\Bigr)^4\right]\unit{\per\hertz}, \nonumber\\
  P_{\rm acc}(f) &= \bigl(\qty{\NoiseAcc}{\femto\metre\per\second\squared}\bigr)^2
  \left[1+\Bigl(\frac{\qty{0.4}{\milli\hertz}}{f}\Bigr)^2\right]
  \left[1+\Bigl(\frac{f}{\qty{8}{\milli\hertz}}\Bigr)^4\right]\unit{\per\hertz},
  \label{eq:noise-single-link}
\end{align}
in the fractional frequency of the response (their eqs.~10 and 13),
\begin{equation}
  S_{\rm oms}(f) = \Bigl(\frac{2\pi f}{c}\Bigr)^2P_{\rm oms}(f),\qquad
  S_{\rm acc}(f) = \frac{P_{\rm acc}(f)}{(2\pi f)^2\,c^2}.
\end{equation}
For equal arms of light time $L = \qty{\NoiseArmSeconds}{\second}$, equal noise levels on every
link and uncorrelated noises, the \acs{tdi}-1.5 channels have the one-sided \acp{psd}
(their eqs.~19 and 127--129, with \acs{tdi}~2.0's common factor $4\sin^22x$ divided out)
\begin{align}
  S_A(f) = S_E(f) &= 8\sin^2x\,\bigl[(2+\cos x)\,S_{\rm oms}
  + 4\,(1 + \cos x + \cos^2 x)\,S_{\rm acc}\bigr], \nonumber\\
  S_T(f) &= 16\sin^2x\,(1-\cos x)\,\bigl[S_{\rm oms} + 2\,(1-\cos x)\,S_{\rm acc}\bigr],
  \qquad x = 2\pi fL,
  \label{eq:noise-psd}
\end{align}
which the simulator draws noise from (\cref{eq:noise-data}), the likelihood weights by and the
network's input is whitened with. Acceleration noise dominates below a few \unit{\milli\hertz}.
Unlike \citet{delmond2026neural} we keep $T$: it is orders of magnitude quieter than $A$ and $E$ at
low frequency, $S_T/S_A = \num{\NoiseTOverALow}$ at \qty{\NoiseBandLow}{\milli\hertz}, but its
signal response is suppressed with it, so it carries almost no \ac{snr} in our band. The noise is instrumental only: no Galactic
confusion foreground \citep{karnesis2021characterization}, no gaps or glitches, and the same
known \acs{psd} in every window.

Signals are compared through the noise-weighted inner product \citep{cutler1994gravitational}
\begin{equation}
  \inner{a}{b} = 4\,\mathrm{Re}\sum_{c}\sum_{k}\frac{a_{k,c}\,b^*_{k,c}}{S_c(f_k)\,\Tobs}
  = 2\,\mathrm{Re}\sum_{c}\sum_{k}\frac{a_{k,c}\,b^*_{k,c}}{P_c(f_k)},
  \label{eq:inner}
\end{equation}
with which the log-likelihood of \cref{eq:loglik} is $-\tfrac12\inner{d-h}{d-h}$ up to a constant,
and the optimal \ac{snr} of a source, \cref{eq:gw-snr}, is $\snr_s = \inner{h(\theta_s)}{h(\theta_s)}^{1/2}$.


We checked the simulator against independent references. The noise generator matches its model
within the Monte-Carlo floor: over \num{\NoiseGeneratorWindows} pure-noise windows every moment of
the whitened noise has its expected value, no correlation between bins or channels and no tilt of the
\acs{psd} across a window exceed the sampling scatter, and $\inner{n}{n}$ follows
$\chi^2_{\NoiseGeneratorDof{}}$ (Kolmogorov--Smirnov $p = \NoiseGeneratorKs{}$). The units are those
of the response: the simulator's sky-averaged sensitivity over the analytic curve of
\citet{robson2019construction} is flat at the expected $1/2$ (\numrange{\NoiseSkyRatioLow}{\NoiseSkyRatioHigh}),
where the noise in fractional length would make it fall as $f^{-2}$. Our \acs{tdi}-1.5 $X$ agrees with
the standard equal-arm $X$ of \citet[eq.~14]{babak2021lisa} built from the same link responses; the
difference, of higher order in $fL$, grows as $f_0^3$ to at most \num{\NoiseTdiMax} at the top of the
band, and the signal powers agree to \qty{\NoiseTdiPowerMax}{\percent}. Finally, for the faint
verification binary of \citet[Table~VII]{strub2022bayesian} our \acs{snr} is \NoiseStrubSnr{} against
their \NoiseStrubSnrTheirs{}, and the reference \acs{mcmc} (\cref{app:mcmc}) under their priors
reproduces their $f_0$ width, their correlations and, over \NoiseStrubRealisations{} noise realisations,
their gain from the observed to the maximum-likelihood \acs{snr}
(\num{\NoiseStrubGain \pm \NoiseStrubGainError} against \NoiseStrubGainTheirs{}). Its $\cos\iota$
interval is wider than theirs; a prior box about the maximum narrows it most of the way, which
suggests their interval reflects a narrower effective prior. On noiseless data every marginal centres
on the truth except $\log_{10}\Amp$ and $\cos\iota$, which slide along their degeneracy with the prior volume.
