\section{Notation}
\label{app:notation}
% =======================================================================================

The appendices follow the pipeline from the data to the results. After the related work
(\cref{app:related}) come the physics: the forward model (\cref{app:forward-model}), the noise and
likelihood (\cref{app:noise}) and the prior (\cref{app:priors}); then the method: the flow
coordinates (\cref{app:chart}), flow matching (\cref{app:fm}), the network (\cref{app:network})
and training (\cref{app:training}); and last the evaluation (\cref{app:evaluation}), the reference
\ac{mcmc} (\cref{app:mcmc}) and the results (\cref{app:results}). \Cref{tab:notation} collects
the symbols used in more than one appendix, the others being defined where they appear, and
\cref{tab:acronyms} the acronyms.
