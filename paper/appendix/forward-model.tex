The forward model maps a source's parameters to the noiseless Fourier coefficients of the \ac{tdi}
channels $A$, $E$, $T$ (\cref{sec:gw-waveforms}); this appendix gives the steps in between.

\subsection{Waveform}
\label{app:forward-model-waveform}

The sky position $(\alpha,\delta)$, in the equatorial frame of the orbit files, defines the
orthonormal triad
\begin{equation}
  \hat{\mathbf u} = (\sin\alpha,\,-\cos\alpha,\,0),\qquad
  \hat{\mathbf v} = (-\sin\delta\cos\alpha,\,-\sin\delta\sin\alpha,\,\cos\delta),\qquad
  \hat{\mathbf k} = (-\cos\delta\cos\alpha,\,-\cos\delta\sin\alpha,\,-\sin\delta),
  \label{eq:forward-model-triad}
\end{equation}
and with it the polarisation tensors of the \ac{ssb},
$\epsilon^+_{ij} = (\hat{\mathbf u}\otimes\hat{\mathbf u} - \hat{\mathbf v}\otimes\hat{\mathbf v})_{ij}$
and
$\epsilon^\times_{ij} = (\hat{\mathbf u}\otimes\hat{\mathbf v} + \hat{\mathbf v}\otimes\hat{\mathbf u})_{ij}$.
The source frame is the \acs{ssb} frame rotated by the polarisation angle $\psi$ about
$\hat{\mathbf k}$: its axes are $\hat{\mathbf p} = \cos\psi\,\hat{\mathbf u} + \sin\psi\,\hat{\mathbf v}$
and $\hat{\mathbf q} = -\sin\psi\,\hat{\mathbf u} + \cos\psi\,\hat{\mathbf v}$, and its polarisation
tensors are
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
  e^+_{ij} &= (\hat{\mathbf p}\otimes\hat{\mathbf p} - \hat{\mathbf q}\otimes\hat{\mathbf q})_{ij}
  = \cos2\psi\,\epsilon^+_{ij} + \sin2\psi\,\epsilon^\times_{ij},\\
  e^\times_{ij} &= (\hat{\mathbf p}\otimes\hat{\mathbf q} + \hat{\mathbf q}\otimes\hat{\mathbf p})_{ij}
  = -\sin2\psi\,\epsilon^+_{ij} + \cos2\psi\,\epsilon^\times_{ij}.
\end{aligned}
  \label{eq:forward-model-source-frame}
\end{equation}
In the \acs{ssb} basis the strain of \cref{eq:forward-model-xi} is therefore
$h_{ij} = h_+^{\rm SSB}\epsilon^+_{ij} + h_\times^{\rm SSB}\epsilon^\times_{ij}$, with
\begin{equation}
  h_+^{\rm SSB} = h_+\cos2\psi - h_\times\sin2\psi,\qquad
  h_\times^{\rm SSB} = h_+\sin2\psi + h_\times\cos2\psi,
  \label{eq:forward-model-ssb-polarisations}
\end{equation}
and $h_{+,\times}$ those of \cref{eq:forward-model-waveform}. In complex form,
$h_{ij}(\xi) = \mathrm{Re}\bigl\{\bigl(A_+\epsilon^+_{ij} + A_\times\epsilon^\times_{ij}\bigr)e^{\ii\Psi(\xi)}\bigr\}$ with
\begin{equation}
  A_+ = -\Amp\bigl[(1+\cos^2\iota)\cos2\psi + 2\ii\cos\iota\sin2\psi\bigr],\qquad
  A_\times = -\Amp\bigl[(1+\cos^2\iota)\sin2\psi - 2\ii\cos\iota\cos2\psi\bigr].
  \label{eq:forward-model-complex-amp}
\end{equation}
Sources quoted in ecliptic coordinates, as in the \acs{lisa} Data Challenges, must be rotated to
this frame.

In the circular basis $\epsilon^{\rm R,L}_{ij} = (\epsilon^+_{ij} \pm \ii\epsilon^\times_{ij})/2$, the two
complex amplitudes of \cref{eq:forward-model-complex-amp} combine into
\begin{equation}
  A_+\epsilon^+_{ij} + A_\times\epsilon^\times_{ij}
  = -\Amp\Bigl[(1+\cos\iota)^2 e^{2\ii\psi}\,\epsilon^{\rm L}_{ij} + (1-\cos\iota)^2 e^{-2\ii\psi}\,\epsilon^{\rm R}_{ij}\Bigr],
  \label{eq:forward-model-helicity}
\end{equation}
so the data depend on $(\Amp,\iota,\psi,\phi_0)$ only through the two complex numbers
$\Amp(1\pm\cos\iota)^2e^{-\ii(\phi_0\mp2\psi)}$, the flow's helicity amplitudes (\cref{eq:chart}),
our counterpart of the amplitude coefficients of \citet{delmond2026neural}.

For component masses $m_1$, $m_2$ at luminosity distance $d_L$, the quadrupole formula fixes the
frequency derivative (\cref{eq:forward-model-fdot}) and the amplitude through the chirp mass
$\Mc = (m_1m_2)^{3/5}/(m_1+m_2)^{1/5}$ \citep{maggiore2008gravitational},
\begin{equation}
  \Amp = \frac{2\,(G\Mc)^{5/3}}{c^4\,d_L}\,(\pi f_0)^{2/3}.
  \label{eq:forward-model-amplitude}
\end{equation}
A measured $\fdot$ gives $\Mc$, and $\Amp$ then $d_L$; otherwise $\Amp$ constrains only
$\Mc^{5/3}/d_L$.

\subsection{Single-Link Response and Heterodyning}
\label{app:forward-model-link}\label{app:forward-model-heterodyne}
% 2026-11-19 (user): merged with the former Heterodyned Frequency-Domain Response subsection.

\ac{lisa} measures the wave as relative frequency fluctuations of six laser links between three
spacecraft. Let $i$ be the receiving and $j$ the emitting spacecraft of link $ij$, $t_i$ the
time of reception and $t_j = t_i - L_{ij}(t_i)$ the time of emission, with $L_{ij}$ the light
travel time. The link response is \citep{finn2009response,cornish2009alternative}
\begin{equation}
  y_{ij}(t_i) = -\frac12\,\frac{H_{ij}(\xi_i) - H_{ij}(\xi_j)}{1 - \hat{\mathbf k}\cdot\hat{\mathbf x}_{ij}},
  \qquad
  H_{ij}(\xi) = \hat x^k_{ij}\hat x^l_{ij}\,h_{kl}(\xi),
  \label{eq:forward-model-link}
\end{equation}
with $\xi_i = t_i - \hat{\mathbf k}\cdot\mathbf x_i(t_i)/c$,
$\xi_j = t_j - \hat{\mathbf k}\cdot\mathbf x_j(t_j)/c$, and $\hat{\mathbf x}_{ij}$ the unit vector
from the emitter to the receiver; \acs{lisa}~\acs{gw}~Response \citep{lisagwresponse} uses the same
response, triad and polarisation convention. For the \ac{gb}, let
$\mathcal A_{ij} = A_+F^+_{ij} + A_\times F^\times_{ij}$, with the antenna patterns
$F^+_{ij} = (\hat{\mathbf u}\cdot\hat{\mathbf x}_{ij})^2 - (\hat{\mathbf v}\cdot\hat{\mathbf x}_{ij})^2$
and $F^\times_{ij} = 2(\hat{\mathbf u}\cdot\hat{\mathbf x}_{ij})(\hat{\mathbf v}\cdot\hat{\mathbf x}_{ij})$, and
\begin{equation}
  \alpha_{ij}(t_i) = \pi L_{ij}\,\bigl(1 - \hat{\mathbf k}\cdot\hat{\mathbf x}_{ij}\bigr)\,
  \bigl(f_0 + \fdot\,\xi_i\bigr),
  \label{eq:forward-model-alpha}
\end{equation}
half the phase the wave advances along the link. If the frequency does not change
appreciably during one light travel time,
\begin{equation}
  y_{ij}(t_i) = -\mathrm{Re}\left\{\frac{\ii\,\mathcal A_{ij}\sin\alpha_{ij}}
  {1 - \hat{\mathbf k}\cdot\hat{\mathbf x}_{ij}}\,
  e^{-\ii\left(\alpha_{ij} - \Psi(\xi_i)\right)}\right\}.
  \label{eq:forward-model-link-gb}
\end{equation}
We make two further approximations: the emitter's position is taken at the time of reception,
$\mathbf x_j(t_j)\simeq\mathbf x_j(t_i)$, so that $\hat{\mathbf x}_{ji} = -\hat{\mathbf x}_{ij}$ and
$\mathcal A_{ji} = \mathcal A_{ij}$; and all six light travel times are equal and constant,
$L_{ij} = L = \qty{\ForwardModelArmGm}{\giga\metre}/c = \qty{\ForwardModelArmSeconds}{\second}$, which puts the
transfer frequency at $f_\star = (2\pi L)^{-1} = \qty{\ForwardModelFStar}{\milli\hertz}$.

With $q = \operatorname{rint}(f_0\Tobs)$ the frequency bin nearest to $f_0$, the response separates
into a carrier and a slowly varying envelope,
$y_{ij}(t) = \mathrm{Re}\bigl\{\mathcal G_{ij}(t)\,e^{2\pi\ii qt/\Tobs}\bigr\}$, with
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal G_{ij}(t) = -\frac{\ii}{2}\,\frac{f_0 + \fdot\,\xi_i}{f_{\star}}\,\mathcal A_{ij}\,
  \operatorname{sinc}\alpha_{ij}\;
  \exp\Bigl\{-\ii\Bigl[\alpha_{ij} + 2\pi f_0\frac{\hat{\mathbf k}\cdot\mathbf x_i(t)}{c}
  - \Bigl(-\phi_0 + 2\pi\bigl(f_0 - \tfrac{q}{\Tobs}\bigr)t + \pi\fdot\,\xi_i^2\Bigr)\Bigr]\Bigr\},
  \label{eq:forward-model-slow}
\end{equation}
where $\operatorname{sinc}x = \sin x/x$; this is \cref{eq:forward-model-link-gb} with the carrier
taken out, since
$\tfrac12(f/f_\star)\operatorname{sinc}\alpha_{ij} = \sin\alpha_{ij}/(1-\hat{\mathbf k}\cdot\hat{\mathbf x}_{ij})$.
The envelope is sampled at the $R$ times $t_m = m\Tobs/R$, $m = 0,\dots,R-1$, and
Fourier-transformed with a rectangular window, which gives the positive-frequency spectrum on
the $R$ bins starting at $k_{\min} = q - R/2$:
\begin{equation}
  \tilde y_{ij}\Bigl(\frac{q+k}{\Tobs}\Bigr) = \frac{\Tobs}{2R}\sum_{m=0}^{R-1}\mathcal G_{ij}(t_m)\,
  e^{-2\pi\ii km/R},\qquad k = -\tfrac R2,\dots,\tfrac R2 - 1.
  \label{eq:forward-model-heterodyne}
\end{equation}
We call $R$ the number of response points. The prefactor makes this the positive-frequency half
of the continuous Fourier transform, to which the noise is normalised (\cref{eq:noise-data}).

The envelope varies on the time scale of \acs{lisa}'s orbit, so its spectrum is confined to a band of
bins around $q$. That band holds three contributions: the annual Doppler sideband,
$\pm f_0v_\oplus/c$, with $v_\oplus = \qty{\ForwardModelVelocity}{\kilo\metre\per\second}$, so
$v_\oplus/c\simeq\num{\ForwardModelVelocityRatio}$; the chirp drift $\fdot\,\Tobs$; and the sidebands of the
antenna pattern, which rotates with the constellation's yearly orbit and modulates the signal at
harmonics of $1/\unit{\year}$ up to the \ForwardModelAntennaOrdinal{} \citep{cutler1998angular}. A source
therefore needs
\begin{equation}
  n_{\rm span}(f_0,\fdot) = 2\left\lceil\Bigl(\frac{f_0 v_\oplus}{c}
  + \fdot\,\Tobs + \frac{\ForwardModelAntennaHarmonics{}}{\unit{\year}}\Bigr)\Tobs\right\rceil \le R
  \label{eq:forward-model-span}
\end{equation}
bins of width $1/\Tobs = \qty{\ForwardModelBinNanoHz}{\nano\hertz}$. Since the outer bins of most
responses lie below the noise, $R$ is set by the bins the data see rather than by this bound
(\cref{fig:forward-model}). Against a \num{\ForwardModelReferencePoints}-point reference, the $R$-bin
response keeps a source's power, with at most \qty{\ForwardModelNarrowTruncOutsideMax}{\percent}
outside its $R$ bins and the \ac{snr} kept to \num{\ForwardModelFullTruncRhoDev}, but not the exact
shape of its spectrum: a rectangular window leaks a tone that falls between bins, and the median
error is \qty{\ForwardModelNarrowTruncRelMedian}{\percent} (narrow) and
\qty{\ForwardModelFullTruncRelMedian}{\percent} (full), below \qty{1}{\percent} on a bin centre and up
to \qty{\ForwardModelNarrowTruncRelOffBin}{\percent} halfway between bins. Training, the test
injections and the reference \ac{mcmc} all use the same response, so the comparison between the flow
and the chains is unaffected.

\subsection{Time-Delay Interferometry}
\label{app:forward-model-tdi}

The laser frequency noise on each link is several orders of magnitude above a \ac{gb}'s signal.
\Ac{tdi} cancels it by combining the six link responses, each delayed by light travel times along
the arms, so that every laser's noise enters twice with opposite signs
\citep{tinto2021time,babak2021lisa}. With the delay operator
\begin{equation}
  D_{i_1i_2\cdots i_n}\,x(t) = x\Bigl(t - \sum_{h=1}^{n-1}L_{i_hi_{h+1}}\Bigr)
  \label{eq:forward-model-delay}
\end{equation}
for constant light travel times $L_{ij}$, the first Michelson combination is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
  X ={}& y_{13} + D_{13}\,y_{31} + D_{131}\,y_{12} + D_{1312}\,y_{21}\\
  &- \bigl[y_{12} + D_{12}\,y_{21} + D_{121}\,y_{13} + D_{1213}\,y_{31}\bigr],
\end{aligned}
  \label{eq:forward-model-tdi-time}
\end{equation}
the difference between the two round trips of spacecraft 1 along its arms, and $Y$ and $Z$ follow
from the cyclic permutation $1\to2\to3\to1$ \citep[eq.~14]{babak2021lisa}. It cancels the laser
noise for a static constellation with unequal arms; for our equal arms it is \acs{tdi}
generation 1.5. In the frequency domain each delay becomes a phase, $D_{ij}\to e^{-2\pi\ii fL_{ij}}$,
by the shift theorem; JaxGB forms the combinations on the envelopes of
\cref{eq:forward-model-slow} and Fourier-transforms them as in \cref{eq:forward-model-heterodyne}.
