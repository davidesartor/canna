\section{The Galactic-Binary Signal and the \acs{lisa} Response}
\label{app:forward-model}
% =======================================================================================

This appendix defines the simulator of \cref{sec:gw}, so that it can be re-implemented: the waveform,
the response of one laser link and its heterodyned form, the \ac{tdi} channels, and the windows the
band is cut into. It ends with the accuracy of the truncated response, which backs the choice of $R$
in \cref{sec:narrow-band}.

\subsection{Waveform}
\label{app:forward-model-waveform}

The transverse-traceless strain of a \ac{gb} at the observer is
\begin{equation}
  h_{ij}(\xi) = h_+(\xi)\,e^+_{ij} + h_\times(\xi)\,e^\times_{ij},\qquad
  \xi = t - \frac{\hat{\mathbf k}\cdot\mathbf x(t)}{c},
  \label{eq:forward-model-xi}
\end{equation}
with $\hat{\mathbf k}$ the direction of propagation, $\mathbf x(t)$ the observer's position and
$e^{+,\times}_{ij}$ the polarisation tensors of the source frame. For a circular binary with a constant
frequency derivative,
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
  h_+(\xi) &= -\Amp\,\bigl(1+\cos^2\iota\bigr)\cos\Psi(\xi),\\
  h_\times(\xi) &= -2\Amp\cos\iota\,\sin\Psi(\xi),\\
  \Psi(\xi) &= -\phi_0 + 2\pi f_0\,\xi + \pi\fdot\,\xi^2.
\end{aligned}
  \label{eq:forward-model-waveform}
\end{equation}
Radiation reaction ties the drift and the amplitude to the chirp mass $\Mc$ and the luminosity distance
$d_L$ \citep{maggiore2008gravitational},
\begin{equation}
  \fdot = \frac{96}{5}\,\pi^{8/3}\left(\frac{G\Mc}{c^3}\right)^{5/3} f_0^{11/3},\qquad
  \Amp = \frac{2\,(G\Mc)^{5/3}}{c^4\,d_L}\,(\pi f_0)^{2/3},
  \label{eq:forward-model-fdot}
\end{equation}
but the simulator keeps $\fdot$ and $\Amp$ free, with the prior of \cref{app:priors-sources}.

The sky position $(\alpha,\delta)$ defines the orthonormal triad
\begin{equation}
  \hat{\mathbf u} = (\sin\alpha,\,-\cos\alpha,\,0),\quad
  \hat{\mathbf v} = (-\sin\delta\cos\alpha,\,-\sin\delta\sin\alpha,\,\cos\delta),\quad
  \hat{\mathbf k} = (-\cos\delta\cos\alpha,\,-\cos\delta\sin\alpha,\,-\sin\delta)
  \label{eq:forward-model-triad}
\end{equation}
in the equatorial frame of the orbit files, with $\epsilon^+ = \hat{\mathbf u}\otimes\hat{\mathbf u} -
\hat{\mathbf v}\otimes\hat{\mathbf v}$ and $\epsilon^\times = \hat{\mathbf u}\otimes\hat{\mathbf v} +
\hat{\mathbf v}\otimes\hat{\mathbf u}$. The source frame is rotated by $\psi$ about $\hat{\mathbf k}$, so
$e^+ = \cos2\psi\,\epsilon^+ + \sin2\psi\,\epsilon^\times$ and
$e^\times = -\sin2\psi\,\epsilon^+ + \cos2\psi\,\epsilon^\times$, and
$h_{ij} = \mathrm{Re}\bigl\{(A_+\epsilon^+_{ij} + A_\times\epsilon^\times_{ij})e^{\ii\Psi}\bigr\}$ with
\begin{equation}
  A_+ = -\Amp\bigl[(1+\cos^2\iota)\cos2\psi + 2\ii\cos\iota\sin2\psi\bigr],\quad
  A_\times = -\Amp\bigl[(1+\cos^2\iota)\sin2\psi - 2\ii\cos\iota\cos2\psi\bigr].
  \label{eq:forward-model-complex-amp}
\end{equation}
In the circular basis $\epsilon^{\rm R,L} = (\epsilon^+ \pm \ii\epsilon^\times)/2$ this is
\begin{equation}
  A_+\epsilon^+ + A_\times\epsilon^\times
  = -\Amp\Bigl[(1+\cos\iota)^2 e^{2\ii\psi}\,\epsilon^{\rm L} + (1-\cos\iota)^2 e^{-2\ii\psi}\,\epsilon^{\rm R}\Bigr],
  \label{eq:forward-model-helicity}
\end{equation}
so the data depend on $(\Amp,\iota,\psi,\phi_0)$ only through the two complex numbers
$\Amp(1\pm\cos\iota)^2e^{-\ii(\phi_0\mp2\psi)}$, the flow's helicity amplitudes (\cref{eq:chart}).

\subsection{Link Response and Heterodyning}
\label{app:forward-model-link}\label{app:forward-model-heterodyne}
% 2026-11-19 (user): merged with the former Heterodyned Frequency-Domain Response subsection.

\ac{lisa} measures relative frequency fluctuations on six laser links. For link $ij$, received at
spacecraft $i$ at time $t_i$ and emitted at $j$ at $t_j = t_i - L_{ij}$, the response is
\citep{finn2009response,cornish2009alternative}
\begin{equation}
  y_{ij}(t_i) = -\frac12\,\frac{H_{ij}(\xi_i) - H_{ij}(\xi_j)}{1 - \hat{\mathbf k}\cdot\hat{\mathbf x}_{ij}},
  \qquad
  H_{ij}(\xi) = \hat x^k_{ij}\hat x^l_{ij}\,h_{kl}(\xi),
  \label{eq:forward-model-link}
\end{equation}
with $\hat{\mathbf x}_{ij}$ the unit vector from emitter to receiver. We take the emitter's position at
the time of reception and all six light travel times equal and constant,
$L = \qty{\ForwardModelArmGm}{\giga\metre}/c$, so the transfer frequency is
$f_\star = (2\pi L)^{-1} = \qty{\ForwardModelFStar}{\milli\hertz}$. With
$\mathcal A_{ij} = A_+F^+_{ij} + A_\times F^\times_{ij}$, where $F^+_{ij}$ and $F^\times_{ij}$ are the
antenna patterns, and $\alpha_{ij} = \pi L(1 - \hat{\mathbf k}\cdot\hat{\mathbf x}_{ij})(f_0 + \fdot\xi_i)$,
the response separates, for $q = \operatorname{rint}(f_0\Tobs)$, into a carrier and a slow envelope,
$y_{ij}(t) = \mathrm{Re}\{\mathcal G_{ij}(t)\,e^{2\pi\ii qt/\Tobs}\}$, with
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal G_{ij}(t) = -\frac{\ii}{2}\,\frac{f_0 + \fdot\,\xi_i}{f_{\star}}\,\mathcal A_{ij}\,
  \operatorname{sinc}\alpha_{ij}\;
  \exp\Bigl\{-\ii\Bigl[\alpha_{ij} + 2\pi f_0\frac{\hat{\mathbf k}\cdot\mathbf x_i(t)}{c}
  - \Bigl(-\phi_0 + 2\pi\bigl(f_0 - \tfrac{q}{\Tobs}\bigr)t + \pi\fdot\,\xi_i^2\Bigr)\Bigr]\Bigr\}.
  \label{eq:forward-model-slow}
\end{equation}
We sample the envelope at $t_m = m\Tobs/R$ and Fourier-transform it with a rectangular window, which
gives the positive-frequency spectrum on the $R$ bins starting at $q - R/2$,
\begin{equation}
  \tilde y_{ij}\Bigl(\frac{q+k}{\Tobs}\Bigr) = \frac{\Tobs}{2R}\sum_{m=0}^{R-1}\mathcal G_{ij}(t_m)\,
  e^{-2\pi\ii km/R},\qquad k = -\tfrac R2,\dots,\tfrac R2 - 1,
  \label{eq:forward-model-heterodyne}
\end{equation}
normalised to the continuous transform that the noise of \cref{eq:noise-data} refers to.

The envelope varies on the time scale of the orbit, so its spectrum is confined to the annual Doppler
sideband $\pm f_0v_\oplus/c$, the chirp drift $\fdot\Tobs$, and the antenna-pattern sidebands at
harmonics of $1/\unit{\year}$ \citep{cutler1998angular}. A source therefore needs
\begin{equation}
  n_{\rm span}(f_0,\fdot) = 2\left\lceil\Bigl(\frac{f_0 v_\oplus}{c}
  + \fdot\,\Tobs + \frac{\ForwardModelAntennaHarmonics{}}{\unit{\year}}\Bigr)\Tobs\right\rceil \le R
  \label{eq:forward-model-span}
\end{equation}
bins, with $v_\oplus = \qty{\ForwardModelVelocity}{\kilo\metre\per\second}$. Most responses fall
below the noise well inside this bound, so $R$ is set by the bins the data see
(\cref{app:forward-model-accuracy}).

\subsection{Time-Delay Interferometry}
\label{app:forward-model-tdi}

\Ac{tdi} cancels the laser frequency noise by combining delayed link responses
\citep{tinto2021time,babak2021lisa}. With the delay operator $D_{i_1i_2\cdots i_n}x(t) =
x(t - \sum_{h}L_{i_hi_{h+1}})$, the first Michelson combination is
\begin{equation}
  X = y_{13} + D_{13}y_{31} + D_{131}y_{12} + D_{1312}y_{21}
  - \bigl[y_{12} + D_{12}y_{21} + D_{121}y_{13} + D_{1213}y_{31}\bigr],
  \label{eq:forward-model-tdi-time}
\end{equation}
and $Y$, $Z$ follow by the cyclic permutation $1\to2\to3\to1$; for equal arms this is \acs{tdi}
generation 1.5. In the frequency domain each delay is a phase $e^{-2\pi\ii fL}$, applied to the
envelopes of \cref{eq:forward-model-slow} before the transform. The noise-orthogonal channels are
\citep{prince2002lisa}
\begin{equation}
  A = \frac{Z-X}{\sqrt2},\qquad E = \frac{X-2Y+Z}{\sqrt6},\qquad T = \frac{X+Y+Z}{\sqrt3}.
  \label{eq:forward-model-aet}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Windows}
\label{app:forward-model-windows}

A window $w$ is $K = \ForwardModelWindowResponses{}R$ consecutive bins with first bin and centre
\begin{equation}
  k_0(\fw) = \ForwardModelWindowStride{}\,\operatorname{rint}\Bigl(\frac{\fw\Tobs - K/2}{\ForwardModelWindowStride{}}\Bigr),
  \qquad \fw = \frac{k_0 + K/2}{\Tobs}.
  \label{eq:forward-model-window-start}
\end{equation}
A source's response fills bins $q-R/2,\dots,q+R/2-1$, which lie inside the window when
$k_0+R/2\le q\le k_0+K-R/2$. Since both bounds are integers this holds whenever $f_0\Tobs$ lies
between them, so a guard band of $R/2$ bins at each edge leaves an interior of
$\ForwardModelInteriorResponses{}R$ bins in which a source belongs to the window,
\begin{equation}
  \max\Bigl(\frac{k_0 + R/2}{\Tobs},\, f_{\min}\Bigr) \le f_0 \le
  \min\Bigl(\frac{k_0 + K - R/2}{\Tobs},\, f_{\max}\Bigr).
  \label{eq:forward-model-window-interior}
\end{equation}
The window index is clipped to the range whose interiors just cover the band.

\subsection{Accuracy of the Truncated Response}
\label{app:forward-model-accuracy}

Against a \num{\ForwardModelReferencePoints}-point reference, the $R$-bin response keeps a source's
power: at most \qty{\ForwardModelNarrowTruncOutsideMax}{\percent} lies outside its $R$ bins and the
\ac{snr} is kept to \num{\ForwardModelFullTruncRhoDev}. It does not keep the exact shape of the
spectrum, because the rectangular window leaks a tone that falls between bins: the median error is
\qty{\ForwardModelNarrowTruncRelMedian}{\percent} (narrow band) and
\qty{\ForwardModelFullTruncRelMedian}{\percent} (full band), below \qty{1}{\percent} on a bin centre
and up to \qty{\ForwardModelNarrowTruncRelOffBin}{\percent} halfway between bins
(\cref{fig:forward-model}). Training, the test injections and the reference \ac{mcmc} share this
response, so the comparison between the flow and the chains is unaffected.
