\begin{abstract}
  % Adapted from the fml draft of 2026-10-11 (origin/fml paper/main.tex) to the current appendices.
  % Each claim and its source: cost (app:fm-sampling, app:mcmc-cost); four sources, full response,
  % bands (app:priors); chart (app:chart); frequency-ordered slots and frame (app:chart-slots, app:fm-frame); network (app:network); simulated
  % batches (app:training-batch); validation power (app:evaluation-validation); references (app:mcmc).
  \Ac{lisa} will resolve thousands of \acp{gb} whose signals overlap in frequency, so their
  parameters must be inferred jointly, which a trans-dimensional \ac{mcmc} global fit does at great
  cost and must repeat for every new stretch of data. We train a conditional flow-matching model
  that returns the joint posterior of the \RelatedWorkSourcesWord{} binaries in a frequency window, under
  the full instrument response and broad priors: \FlowMatchingCostDraws{} draws take
  \qty{\FlowMatchingCostNarrow}{\second} on one GPU, where a joint \ac{mcmc} chain of the same
  window takes \qtyrange{\McmcCostJointLo}{\McmcCostJointHi}{\hour} on \McmcJointCores{} CPU cores.
  The flow runs on straight paths in a flat chart of eight coordinates per source, in which the
  signal is linear in one complex amplitude per helicity, so the four equivalent copies of each
  source's polarisation and phase become one mode. The sources are slots in order of frequency, so
  each mode has one copy rather than one per labelling. From the data alone the network returns a
  Gaussian frame per source, trained on the likelihood of the true sources, and the flow carries a
  standard normal to the posterior in that frame, so loud sources, whose posteriors are decades
  narrower than the prior, reach the flow with widths of order one. A transformer reads the data as time--frequency tokens in a stream that depends
  on the observation alone, encoded once per window, and every training batch is simulated on the
  device, so no observation is seen twice. We train on the band below
  \qty{\PriorsNarrowHighMhz}{\milli\hertz} with windows of \PriorsNarrowW{} bins and on the whole
  band, up to \qty{\PriorsFullHighMhz}{\milli\hertz}, with windows of \PriorsFullW. To validate the
  flow without the truth, we show on a toy with an exact posterior that ranks of the parameters
  miss phase, sky and dependence errors that keep every one-dimensional rank uniform, while ranks of data-space
  quantities, the overlap and the log-likelihood, catch them; joint and per-source \ac{mcmc} chains,
  which agree with each other except where neighbours overlap strongly, give the reference widths.

\end{abstract}
