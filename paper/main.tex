\ac{gb} prior.

\section*{Software and Data}
The code is at \url{https://github.com/davidesartor/canna}: the simulator, network, training
and evaluation, and the scripts that make every number, figure and table in this paper.
It is written in JAX \citep{bradbury2018jax} with Equinox \citep{kidger2021equinox}, Optax
\citep{deepmind2020jax} and Orbax \citep{gaffney2026orbax}. The \ac{gb} response is JaxGB's
\citep{jaxgb} on the orbits of LISA Orbits \citep{lisaorbits,martens2021trajectory}, and the
reference chains run on jexplore \citep{jexplore}.

\section*{Impact Statement}

This paper presents work whose goal is to advance the field of Machine Learning. There
are many potential societal consequences of our work, none which we feel must be
specifically highlighted here.
