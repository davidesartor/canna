\begin{abstract}
  % Draft of 2026-10-11, from the appendices and the related-work survey (app:related). Each
  % claim and its source: one second per XS window (app:mcmc, cost); four sources, full
  % response, broad priors (app:priors); the set coupling (app:geometry); 127/128 and
  % 1-2.6x for isolated found sources (tab:mcmc); 25x ideal (tab:xs-final); fourfold
  % (tab:xs-ladder, 0.147 -> 0.037 bins); the same precision on XS and B (app:b); 3.5-fold
  % by mid-training with position features (R1, app:b).
  LISA will resolve some $10^4$ galactic binaries whose signals overlap in frequency, so their
  parameters must be inferred jointly, which a trans-dimensional MCMC global fit does at great
  cost and must repeat for every new stretch of data. We train a conditional flow-matching
  model that returns, in about a second per window (under a minute for the widest windows), the
  joint posterior of four overlapping binaries in a frequency window, under the full instrument response and broad priors. The flow
  transports the prior to the posterior on the product manifold of the sources' parameters
  (intervals, spheres and a circle) and treats the sources as an unordered set: each target is
  aligned to its prior draw over the permutations of the sources, so the symmetry of the labels
  never enters the learned velocity field. Against a joint 32-dimensional MCMC, the MCMC median
  lies inside the flow's 95\% interval for 127 of 128 source--parameter pairs, and isolated
  sources the flow finds are within one to three times the MCMC width; but loud sources stop
  at a floor of 25 times the ideal width in frequency, and faint sources next to loud ones are
  missed. We show that the flow-matching loss is nearly blind to this floor, that a warped time
  schedule and a learning-rate cooldown lower it fourfold, and that what remains is set by how
  the network represents where a source sits: the same precision in coordinate units on windows
  64 times wider that cover the whole band, which Fourier features of the positions of sources
  and data cut 3.5-fold by mid-training. \tbd[ R1 and B2: the final numbers]%
  \draftnote{Draft abstract (2026-10-11), revised after the paper review; the last sentence
  waits for R1's cooldown and B2.}
\end{abstract}
